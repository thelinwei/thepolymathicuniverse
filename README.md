# The Polymathic Universe

**Worlds built for fiction still to be written.** The companion to [The Polymathic Learner](https://thelinwei.github.io/thepolymathiclearner/), which holds the academic work.

**Live:** https://thelinwei.github.io/thepolymathicuniverse/

## The Parallel Straits

An exploratory atlas of a parallel equatorial port and its seven great schools, kept as a working record to revisit and build from. The volumes are numbered from 00 in the order they were made and sorted into five books.

- **Book One · The Design**: what the seven schools wear, mark, build and teach. Fashion, identity, architecture, education.
- **Book Two · World Building**: when, where, who, what happened, how they live, and the world told as stories.
- **Book Three · Magic System**: the rules, spells, instruments, fighting arts, great works, casters and origins.
- **Book Four · To Further Investigate**: the real-world research beneath the world, with links to follow.
- **Book Five · P.S. Note to Self**: reflective essays written along the way.

Open `index.html` for the full index. Each volume is labelled by the hand that drafted it: Claude, ChatGPT or DeepSeek.
