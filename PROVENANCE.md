## Declaration of independent creation

I conceived, directed and designed the world in this repository: its premise, its seven schools, its histories, its magic and its names. It is independent creative work, made in my personal time on personal equipment, using tools and services I paid for myself. It was not commissioned, sponsored or funded by any institution or employer, and it forms no part of any institutional research programme.

Claude (Anthropic) and ChatGPT (OpenAI) took part as sustained collaborators, with smaller parts from DeepSeek and Grok, through accounts I held personally. Each volume is labelled on the index by the hand that drafted it. The direction, selection, naming, editing and final form of every volume are mine.

— Lin Wei, 15 November 2026
