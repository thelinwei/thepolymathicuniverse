[The Sensing Years]暗流 · 一九二一至一九四六
(twenty-five years between the Congress and the Covenant: the craft spreading under licence, the push and shove of every partner, the second Congress, the Narrows and one night of war, and the first circle coming home to the table)

Twenty-five years of reading one another, and one night that settled the reading.

How to read itOne spine, two sides

Time runs down the spine. The Straits is on the left and the world on the right. Events that move both cross the spine and span the page.

The StraitsThe worldBothA blank to fill

The Congress ended in a promise and a test. The Straits would teach; the schools of the first circle would show, over twenty-five years, what they did with what they were taught. Nobody in 1921 knew what that would look like, the seven houses least of all. They were idealists who believed the knowledge belonged to everyone, and realists who had watched the cleverest delegates turn pale in the silent night.

So the years that followed were years of sensing. Teachers went out in pairs, a healer beside a fighter, and taught the craft like reading. Partner schools learned, and pushed: for the workings they had glimpsed and not been sold, for the right to teach onward, for fewer letters home. Some partners kept faith. Some were dropped when their rulers showed what they really wanted. Most of what passed between the Straits and the world in those years happened below the surface, in letters, visits and refusals.

Meanwhile magic went into the streets. Artefacts that once lasted a month began to last six. The houses built workshops, makers abroad copied them, and a fishwife at Kilwa kept her fish cold till evening. When the second Congress opened the sale of artefacts in 1934, power could be bought as well as learned, and a port at the gate of the Baltic decided to take what it could not buy.

What Veyr did to the teachers it seized, and what the Straits did in reply on a single night in the Sound, settled the question the Congress had asked. When the first circle came home to the table in 1946, the world knew what the Straits would and would not do with magic. The Covenant sealed what twenty-five years had proved.

Movement i · 1921–1927

The Licensed Craft 执照十年

The teachers go out, the craft spreads, and the first partners are tested.

The StraitsThe world
1921–1922
The Straits · a.m. 3–4

The Pairing伴行

The first teachers sail with the first circle’s delegations. Every teacher sent abroad must be able to defend themselves to a set standard, and every non-combat specialist, a healer, a grower, a water-reader, travels with a combat mage. Teachers go. Students stay home.

The world · a.m. 3–4

The first classrooms

Partner schools clear rooms, build benches and send their best to learn. They find what the bid paper promised: the Straits teaches, and whether anyone learns is up to them.

1921–1931
The Straits and the world · a.m. 3–13

The licensed craft执照十年

The Straits

The Straits writes its first grammars and songbooks. Everyday workings are taught like reading. About one adult in five can learn a simple spell, one in twenty an advanced one. The first artefacts last a month, and Renewal Day comes round in every school yard.

The world

Wherever the craft lands, it begins to remake work and streets the way industry did in our world, port by port and school by school. Kite-ferries cross the Straits; the first parallax mills turn under its bridges.

A rivet boy drives his first glowing rivet with frost round his boots, and the whole gang downs tools to throw snowballs.

1922–1926
The Straits · a.m. 4–8

The weighing goes on

Every new approach meets the same five questions and the same weighing. The houses learn to read a bid quickly, and to read the delegate who carries it.

The world · a.m. 4–8

The second wave

Schools and powers that were not invited, or ignored the letter, sail to the Straits to bid. Some come in good faith. Some come to learn how the bidding works.

Mid-1920s
The Straits

The invasion turned back

A foreign power comes to take by force what it would not bid for. In four years the houses have built enough magical strength and trained enough people to turn it back, and they do.

1920s
The Straits

The first drops

A partner whose rulers turn their teaching to private gain or open ambition is dropped from the circle. Its teachers are recalled, its gifts left in its keeping. The Straits is idealist about sharing and clear-eyed about what happens when sharing goes unchecked.

The world

Resentment, and a rumour

The dropped call it a cartel. The word travels, and it will be remembered in the Narrows.

Movement ii · 1927–1934

Workshops and the Second Congress 工坊 · 再会

Magic goes into the street, and then onto the market.

The StraitsThe world
1927–1934
The Straits and the world · a.m. 9–16

Undercurrents暗流

The Straits

The articles of the Covenant are argued out case by case, by letter and by visit. The Straits holds its conditions: consent, permitted uses, the return of findings, the safety of teachers.

The world

The partner schools push back on every side. They want the workings they glimpsed in the silent night. They want to teach onward without asking. They want fewer reports. Most of it never reaches a page anyone outside the schools can read.

1931–1950
The Straits · a.m. 13–32

The workshops工坊

Artefacts now last six months, and each house founds its workshop: Sund heat, Toa Keng optics, Sané-Kora sound, Sri Temenggong living ecology, Nine Rain stone and water, Al-Merikani finance, Lan Fang making and medicine. Their marks become the marks people trust.

A renewer’s tricycle on every street, its bell ringing before the lamps run down.

The world · a.m. 13–32

In the Sund manner

Paired schools and independent makers build their own artefacts, often very well, and sell them “in the Sund manner” or “after Lan Fang”. The Long Song links Timbuktu and the Straits in a day. The old caravan cities bloom on the trade in expendables.

A fishwife at Kilwa hangs a rain cord and a cold tile over her stall, and her fish stay cold till evening.

Apr 1934
The Straits and the world · a.m. 16

The second Congress再会

The Straits

Twelve and a half years after the first, at the other turn of the monsoon, the Straits calls a second Congress: for new members, for the renewal of old ones, and for the sale of artefacts. Its Covenant falls due in 1959.

The world

Buying power ready-made is quicker than learning it. Ports that would never pass the five questions can still buy what the questions guard. The seeds of an arms race are sown at an auction.

Movement iii · 1934–1939

The Narrows 狭海

A toll port decides to take what it cannot buy, and is answered in one night.

The StraitsThe world
1930s
The world

Veyr at the Narrows狭海之维尔

Veyr has lived for centuries on the dues of every ship passing the Narrows of the Sound, the gate of the Baltic. Wind-roads now let ships sail past without stopping, and its trade is failing. Exiled engineers and officers bring a creed with them: advanced learning is a Straits cartel, and a port that cannot break things will be broken. Three times Veyr asks for licences to compose harbour-breaking workings, and three times it is refused. Its guard boards Concord ships and returns their artefacts broken open. In a single night of votes and threats, an arsenal faction takes the council.

1930s
The Straits and the world

The Empty Desks空桌

The Straits

Every chair the dead left stays empty for a full academic year. On the anniversary, every school in the port teaches one lesson without speaking: the Veyr Silence, 维尔之默.

The world

Straits teachers on exchange in Veyr, with their combat pairs, are seized and tortured for destructive magic. They had taught shipyard rings, fever medicine, star-fixing, rain cords, law. None of them gives what Veyr wants, and they die there. Veyr steals their books and learns to run gentle recipes backwards, sinking the burden into other people’s land.

1930s
The Straits

The Senyap Works静岛工坊

On a quiet island in the eastern Straits the seven houses gather their composers, chaired by the 巨子 of Mozi College. The children’s martial classes become corps. The doctrine, argued for a year: unmake, do not kill. The first test, the Hush, overreaches, and for one hour the whole port lives as the world did before the Kindling.

The Manhattan Project, 1942–1946.

The world

The Closed Harbour War

Veyr turns its reversed recipes on its neighbours, invades and pillages other ports, and calls its conquests humane because few die on the field.

Late 1930s
The Straits and the world

The Veyr Retaliation雷霆

The Straits

Five founders cast their 绝学 together: Tesslund silences Veyr’s coupled systems, Konggu closes its war council, Beek holds its army between two beats, Hua He puts its garrisons to sleep, Guna Watu lets the stone of its fortresses go.

The world

Fought as the Battle of the Sound. In one night a fleet is unbuilt in silence, its rulers sleep, its stolen knowledge is taken back and its land is left to rest. Fewer than three hundred die. A new magical world order begins the next morning.

After the Sound
The Straits

Students go abroad游学

Only now do Straits students sail to partner schools, the two-way exchange the Congress always promised.

The world

A world that has seen it

Every court and council has heard what one night can do. The circle’s membership grows, and so does its weight.

Movement iv · 1940–1946

Home to the Table 发馨合盟

The circle comes home, and each school is measured as it sits down.

The StraitsThe world
1940s
The Straits · a.m. 22–28

Planning for absence

Every delegation had left the Congress with several gifts to tend: seeds, waters, cured meats, a living starter. If one cannot come home, another still can. The Straits plans the Covenant for the circle it hopes for, and for the one it will actually get.

The world · a.m. 22–28

The tending

Seeds are resown each season, starters fed, waters raised, findings written up in letters. Some polities change, and some do not survive the decade. Some schools are still under assessment, held back until they meet the conditions, and resent it.

1946
The Straits and the world · a.m. 28

The Covenant发馨合盟

The Straits

The first circle comes home to the table. Recognition, returned knowledge, the opening, the shared table, renewal, further entrusting. The articles argued out over twenty-five years are sealed, and the Covenant of Magic stands.

The world

Each school is measured as it sits down. At the least it did no harm and kept its word. At its best it brings back new knowledge, eagerness and gratitude, and asks for more to explore.

The United Nations is founded, 1945.
