Thread III

Words of the world

The current meaning of each term.

a.m.魔后

After magic, counted from the Kindling: 1918 is a.m. 1, 2026 is a.m. 109. Written in italics.

Everyday workings日常术

The small magic everyone can learn, the way everyone can learn to read.

The ladder阶

Simple spells like a diploma (one in five at first, three in five now), advanced like a degree, mastery like a doctorate, composers like post-doctoral research.

Gift天赋

How far training can take a person, 0 to 5. Read as flair as much as ceiling.

Narrow and deep专而深

A Gift that runs in one domain only, often very strong; many dyslexic, autistic and differently wired people. Found through the Seven Doors.

Artefact法器

A made thing holding a spell anyone can use. Renewed: a month, six months, a year, three years for the schools’ own. Heirlooms last generations.

Instrument法具

An amplifier a caster may carry; preferred, never required. Five grades, from bare hands to legendary.

Expendable耗材

An ingredient the recipe uses up. Essential, cheap, in every market.

Medicine药

What each house brews for the body, taken rather than cast with: Lan Fang’s 丹药, Sund’s cod-liver oil, Sri Temenggong’s jamu, Nine Rain’s cinchona bark.

Burden and sink负担 · 汇

The weight a working leaves, and the place it lands. Sinks need consent under the Charter.

The Chain of Keeping证链

Al-Merikani’s ledger, sealed by many hands in many ports; it proves who held what, and nothing on it can be quietly changed. The Witness Chain, on the quays.

The Uncut Seat不割之席

In every great work, a seat for the people of the ground with the power to say no. Modelled on the Guna Council at the Stair.

The Empty Desks空桌

The schools’ name for the Veyr Exchange Murders: every chair the dead left stays empty for a year.

The Monsoon Charter季风宪章

The rules of engagement for war and trade; its rules are the Straits Articles. Kept in the Charter Hall; members from beyond the Straits hold the majority.

The green kidney绿肾

A cultivated wetland that cleans a town’s water.

Two hands, one event

Where Claude’s and GPT’s volumes tell the same thing, and which name canon keeps. Both volumes stay as they were made.

The aggressor

Claude Stettin, the coal court of the Oder (93, first draft)
GPT Veyr at the Narrows (99)
Canon Veyr, a toll port at the Narrows of the Sound. The Silesian Breaking is the precedent: Konrad’s scattered court brings its grievance there, and its arsenal faction takes the council.

The killings

Claude The Stettin Silence (93, first draft)
GPT The Veyr Exchange Murders; the Empty Desks (99)
Canon Three names: the Veyr Exchange Murders in the courts, the Empty Desks in the schools, the Veyr Silence for the anniversary lesson.

The war

Claude The Battle of the Sound (93)
GPT The Closed Harbour War, in five phases (99)
Canon The Closed Harbour War, ending at the Battle of the Sound.

The arsenal

Claude Eight workings (93)
GPT Sixteen spell cards (99)
Canon One arsenal. The Long Sleep and the Quiet Crown are cousins; so are Ground Says No and the Bitter Ground.

The crash

Claude The Three Exchanges and Clean Paper (93)
GPT The Long Account and Burden Notes; a weak monsoon as trigger (99)
Canon The Three Exchanges run their bundles through the Long Account; a weak monsoon pulls the first thread.

Holding steady

Claude The Great Ring (93)
GPT The World Holds One Night (99)
Canon The Great Ring ends on the Ninth Night with the World Holds One Night, and the Ninth Night lamp begins there.

The rules

Claude The Monsoon Charter (93)
GPT The Straits Articles (99)
Canon The Monsoon Charter; its rules are the Straits Articles, with 99’s twelve as the full text.

Suez

Claude The Two Seas Cut (95)
GPT The Canal of the Two Waters (100)
Canon The Canal of the Two Waters; pilots call it the Two Seas Cut.

Panama

Claude The Isthmus Stair (95)
GPT The Stair of Two Oceans; the Uncut Seat (100)
Canon The Stair of Two Oceans; sailors call it the Isthmus Stair. The Guna seat is the first Uncut Seat.

Ocean roads

Claude The Glide Lanes (95)
GPT The Blue Lines and the Monsoon Express (100)
Canon The Blue Lines.

The ledger

Claude The Witness Chain (95)
GPT The Chain of Keeping (100)
Canon The Chain of Keeping; the Witness Chain on the quays.

The martial arts

Claude Seven signature arts (96)
GPT Seven theories of victory, with named forms (100)
Canon Both: 96 for each house’s art, 100 for the forms and Lan Fang’s two-speed taichi.

Reading the GPT volumes

They stay as they were made; here is how to read them against canon.

86 How Power GrowsGPT

Uses the older Gift picture. Read through the ladder and narrow-and-deep.

87 Magic with a Day JobGPT

Names drift: Sund Commons, Nine Rains, Kora, Sri Temmengong. Read as Sund, Nine Rain, Sané-Kora, Sri Temenggong.

88 The Battlefield Learns a TradeGPT

Combat lineages sit beside 96 The Gentle Way; no conflict.

89 The Martial StraitsGPT

Same name drift as 87; labelled exploratory. 96 adds each house’s signature art.

99 When the Schools Took Up ArmsGPT

GPT’s telling of the war and the crash. Its Veyr is canon; 93 places it at the Narrows of the Sound. Same name drift as 87, and TKI for Toa Keng.

100 The Great Works and Seven Theories of VictoryGPT

GPT’s canals, world works, ledger and martial theories. Its six extra works join canon beside 95’s seven.

GPT’s A Different Industrial CenturyGPT

Places the First Door in 1937 and adds a Threshold Accord in 1948. Canon keeps 1983. Its ideas live on in 92.
