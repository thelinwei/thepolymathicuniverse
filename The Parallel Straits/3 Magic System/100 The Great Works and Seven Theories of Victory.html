I

The Great Works

Magic becomes infrastructure when wonder survives its opening ceremony: maintained by ordinary workers, renewed on schedule, governed by those who live beside it and answerable for where every displaced burden goes.

02

The two Great Cuttings

One canal negotiates sand, salinity and two seas. The other negotiates mountain, forest, drinking water and rain. They must not be the same magical trick.

Eastern work · sea-level passage

The Canal of the Two Waters

Across Concord-aligned Egypt, the Inner Sea is joined to the Red Sea. Sund opens controlled planes of shear; Lan Fang grades and recomposes every displaced material; Nine Rains stages the meeting of tides and salinities; Sri grows the banks that masonry cannot keep alive alone.

“No earth disappears. It changes custody.”
  1. Plural survey. TKI maps settlement, aquifer, caravan, burial ground, ecology and navigation separately before proposing a shared line.
  2. Shear opening. Sund makes layers slide cleanly rather than exploding them; workers remain beside the moving ground.
  3. Useful spoil. Lan Fang turns stone into quays, clay into bricks and sand into berms and harbour foundations.
  4. Meeting of waters. Nine Rains opens the seas through graduated basins so salinity and sediment do not collide at once.
  5. Living edge. Sri establishes reeds, salt gardens, root-bound slopes and animal crossings.
  6. Covenanted passage. Al Merikani prevents any single fleet or treasury from owning the route.
Western work · watershed staircase

The Stair of Two Oceans

Across the American isthmus, vessels borrow elevation from a living watershed. Nine Rains composes lakes, rain basins and slow-release wetlands; Lan Fang’s locks flex as articulated joints; Sund gives saturated slopes safe paths along which to yield.

“A vessel may borrow elevation. It may not purchase the watershed.”
  1. Consent before alignment. Isthmus communities and Shore Leagues identify what cannot be cut, flooded, priced or moved.
  2. Rain as infrastructure. Nine Rains reserves water for people, forest and drought before assigning any to shipping.
  3. Jointed locks. Lan Fang gates replace individual members while the larger machine remains operational.
  4. Listening slopes. Sri forests disclose strain through root, fungal and animal behaviour before instruments see failure.
  5. Audible operation. Kora gives each approach, closure, rise and release a rhythm; a mistimed chamber can be heard.
  6. Water held in trust. Merikani allocates passages conditionally; wealth alone cannot buy the last water of a dry season.
03

Seven schools, one work

No school owns a planetary project. Each supplies an indispensable faculty—and an equally indispensable objection to everybody else.

Toa Keng

Plural survey

Measures alignment, depth, risk and use through several frames. Every number carries standpoint, season, confidence, author and expiry.

Al Merikani

Custody & terms

Holds land, payment, passage and burden in conditional trust. Makes authority visible and revocable.

Lan Fang

Recomposition

Builds joints, gates, dredgers and renewal systems. Makes displaced matter useful instead of magically absent.

Sund

Shear & commons

Moves enormous loads through lateral yielding, shares stress and makes maintenance part of the original structure.

Nine Rains

Hydrological feedback

Coordinates current, rain, tide, pressure, salinity and sediment without pretending to control the whole climate.

Sri

Living continuity

Works directly with roots, forests, reefs and animals so the cut remains inhabitable by more than ships.

Kora

Coordination & witness

Synchronises crews, makes failures audible and preserves the songs, losses and testimony that a technical record omits.

TKI may determine the shortest line. The project does not begin until the others ask: shortest for whom, in which season, across whose ground, and at whose continuing cost?

05

The Blue Lines

High-speed rail, translated into an oceanic civilisation: not rails on water and not a faster private engine, but a scheduled public right-of-way made from currents, anchors, weather and trust.

The Monsoon Express

  • Seabed shear-nodes, floating stations and harbour thresholds braid a stable travelling corridor.
  • Convoys share wave impact, navigational certainty, cooling and rescue capacity.
  • Opposing traffic occupies separate current-braids; migration routes and fishing grounds become mandatory bends.
  • Repeated journeys make ports relationally closer, but public subsidy prevents wealthy routes from monopolising proximity.
  • Disabled vessels remain buoyant and retrievable—why water, not air, becomes mass intercontinental transport.
“Kilwa, Aden, Gujarat, the Straits and Quanzhou become consecutive stations.”

Mohist kites carry news, surgeons, surveyors, rescue workers and daring individuals. They do not carry grain. News flies. Grain sails.

Not bigger spells, but institutions that hold a useful relation across continents, oceans or generations.

Renewal houses at Concord harbours allow artefacts made in one tradition to be inspected and renewed abroad without reducing every recipe to one grammar.

Sund Commons

Sri

Kora

07

Al Merikani’s Chain of Keeping

Magical blockchain—hahahaha, yes—but built from distributed custody and witnessed obligation rather than computational proof.

01 · Making

Origin witnessed

Maker, recipe family, materials and first burden enter independent keeping-houses.

02 · Passage

Custody changes

Every transfer is signed by giver, receiver, carrier and the conditions of release.

03 · Renewal

Life extended

Inspection, repairs, altered sinks and expiry are appended without erasing earlier states.

04 · Condition

Promise executes

Payment or access releases only when several independent witnesses agree.

05 · Burden

Cost remains named

No obligation may be bundled so completely that its final human or ecological sink disappears.

TKI: What exactly counts as agreement?
Kora: A consistent record can preserve a lie.
Sri: Living relationships cannot be frozen at entry.
Nine Rains: Diffuse consequences refuse one transaction.
Sund: Who records the labour keeping this chain alive?
Lan Fang: Every executable promise will eventually be exploited as a machine.

The Chain proves continuity of custody. It does not prove truth, goodness or justice. That confusion will one day help create the Burden Notes.

II

Seven Theories of Victory

The body, atmosphere, rhythm, instrument, condition and law become martial media. The mature schools compete not merely over who can win, but over what kind of ending deserves to be called victory.

08

Sri and Nine Rains finally separate

Both grew from ecological and water histories. Their causal relationships are now distinct enough that their grand works—and their fighting—no longer blur.

Sri · organism to practitioner

Engage the living being

Sri works directly through cultivated relationships with plants and, more selectively, animals. A mangrove may seize a boot; roots may disclose strain; birds may warn; an animal may choose to obstruct or retrieve.

  • Plants answer more readily than animals.
  • Animals are allies, never disposable summoned weapons.
  • Power deepens through long familiarity with a place.
  • The signature victory: the living place refuses the aggressor.
Nine Rains · condition to system

Compose the surrounding conditions

Nine Rains does not command the mangrove. It changes moisture, wind, current, pressure, temperature, mist, friction and the timing of rain through which every organism moves.

  • Acts farther away but less personally.
  • Begins fighting before the opponent arrives.
  • Excels at terrain, visibility and collective movement.
  • The signature victory: favourable conditions cease to exist.
Sri asks the forest. Nine Rains asks the weather in which the forest must answer.

The seven schools on the fighting floor

Filter the field atlas by school. These are directions for further composition, not exclusive elements or settled canon.

Lan Fang · healer’s hand

The body has many answers

“We seem gentle because we know exactly what violence does.”

Taichi’s circularity and two-speed training meet Aikido’s entering, blending and joint control, then pass through Lan Fang medicine. The same hand can restore, redirect, immobilise—or end a life.

The Courteous Sleeve

Enter beside an attack, turn with it and fold the attacker gently to the floor without stopping their movement by impact.

The physician perceives seven possible endings—release, numbness, sleep, stillness, unconsciousness, injury and death—and chooses the least harmful one that will work.

The Last Meridian

The forbidden knowledge: where an insignificant touch becomes fatal. Its terror lies in how rarely a Lan Fang master needs it.

Sri · allied living world

The place joins the encounter

“No one fights alone on living ground.”

Silat-derived low movement, performance, keris and disarmament combine with direct relations to roots, plants and willing animals.

Root Beneath the Heel

A familiar root rises exactly enough to interrupt a committed step, then settles without breaking the soil.

The Animal’s Refusal

Birds expose concealment, macaques steal a dropped instrument or a herd closes a path—but only where an existing relationship makes answering plausible.

The Keris Returns Empty

Vines and sash redirect the weapon while the practitioner leaves the attacker uncut.

Nine Rains · atmospheric field

Remove favourable conditions

“The duel began when the air changed.”

Weather rather than organism: microclimate, water film, wind, humidity, current, pressure and visibility make the field itself fluid.

Wet Sole

A film of water changes friction for one pivot—effortless for the practitioner, treacherous for the pursuing foot.

Crosswind Needle

A tiny pressure difference bends a projectile away without requiring a visible wall.

Rain Before Arrival

Ground is saturated in selected paths an hour before conflict. Heavy formations discover too late that their own weight is the trap.

The Army Without Weather

Mist removes range, damp breaks grip, wind scatters sound and heat refuses to leave armour. Nobody is struck; coordinated advance becomes impossible.

Kora · the last verse

Every action must answer

“Your formation has no next beat.”

Rap battle, praise poetry, call-and-response and musical cutting contest become quiet and lethal. A duel may be two people speaking softly while a courtyard loses the ability to move.

The Answering Step

Each movement must answer the previous one. A combatant unable to answer becomes motionless for one beat.

The Name Beneath the Name

The singer names the role being performed—hero, martyr, victim—and then the private motive beneath it. The opponent’s cadence falters.

No Chorus Answers You

The assembly withdraws rhythmic recognition. A commander can still shout, but nobody feels where the orders belong.

The Last Verse

Both fighters know the next complete line ends the encounter. They circle in silence, each trying to make the other begin it.

Energy must go somewhere

Al Merikani · understood wager

The condition precedes the strike

“You accepted before you understood what you had staked.”

Speed is only one possible cowboy condition. A duel may be decided by who looks away, repeats a movement, becomes angry, speaks falsely or acts before understanding.

The Courtesy Before Violence

Object placement, distance, posture and gaze propose possible rules before either party declares a duel.

The Unsaid Wager

A condition binds only when both parties genuinely recognise it. One person cannot secretly invent a rule for the other.

The Empty Holster

The practitioner stakes the weapon itself. Victory without drawing releases everything entrusted to it.

The Duel Already Lost

A master manoeuvres the opponent into wagering the one thing their character will not permit them to surrender.

Toa Keng · archer & referee

Measure until injustice aligns every frame

“This encounter has become unfair. It stops now.”

TKI produces the finest archers and magical snipers: patient observers of distance, trajectory, uncertainty and the single point where several descriptions briefly agree. Their greatest force appears when obvious unfairness collapses parallax into moral certainty.

The Arrow of Least Assumption

The shot follows the trajectory requiring the fewest unverified claims.

The Witness at Twenty Miles

The sniper perceives not the body but the moment a distant actor commits to an action.

Objection Sustained

An unlawful spell freezes halfway through its recipe; its caster becomes immobilised at the exact point of violation.

The Court Descends

A temporary jurisdiction forms: no attack on the unarmed, no pursuit after surrender, no concealed burden and no prevention of testimony. The harder one violates it, the more completely one binds oneself.

10

Lan Fang’s two-speed civic art

Taichi is not intrinsically super slow. Relaxed circularity, breath, five steps, eight techniques and pushing hands can be taught across ages; martial practice also contains changes of tempo, hard–soft alternation and explosive release.

Public form

Slow enough for everyone

Schools, clinics and parks teach alignment, breath, memory, recovery and shared movement. Slowness makes the relation visible.

Partner form

Listen through contact

Pushing hands develops sensitivity to intention, tension, balance and the instant force changes direction.

Physician form

Choose the body’s answer

The sequence becomes diagnostic: touch discovers where movement, circulation or breath has ceased to cooperate.

Combat form

Coil, enter, release

The apparently gentle phrase compresses into a rapid redirection, lock or explosive issue of force at one precise vulnerability.

The elderly person in the park and the Lan Fang master in a duel may be practising the same form. What changes is not the grammar, but speed, compression, intention and how much of the body’s answer the practitioner is prepared to invoke.

Research anchors: UNESCO describes Taijiquan through relaxed circular movement, breath, five steps, eight techniques and pushing hands; the International Wushu Federation notes slow movement coupled at times with explosive bursts of force.

11

Four easily confused powers

The schools become clearest where their methods almost overlap.

Lan Fang

Body knowledge

Controls the anatomical and energetic sequence by which action becomes possible.

The hand decides which human response arrives.

Al Merikani

Mutual condition

Two parties enter a specific wager, sometimes without speaking its mutually understood terms.

The commitment creates the consequence.

Kora

Answer and cadence

A person or formation loses the rhythm, story or communal recognition required for its next action.

There is no next beat to inhabit.

Toa Keng

Public adjudication

A rule becomes binding because an encounter has crossed into legible unfairness, not because opponents privately wagered it.

The violation writes its own restraint.
