Fourteen familiar fantasy power-classes passed through seven incompatible-but-functional civilisational grammars — plus the powers that only appear once those grammars are allowed to evolve beyond the inherited superhero catalogue.

The test is simple: Can they do it? How do they think they do it? What does centuries of institutional specialisation turn it into?

§0 · HOW TO READ THE ATLAS

Do not ask “who owns telepathy?”

Every School can, in principle, reach most domains. The question is which path feels native, what resistance costs it carries, and what centuries of institutional research have made unusually sophisticated.

A classic fantasy power is only the manifest layer. The deeper comparison is between causal architectures.

POWER 01 · Force · Matter

Fire & Lightning

Classical repertoire: fireballs, lightning, heat, light, explosive force

Operations: Create · Transform · Move
TKIParallax

Reframes where heat or charge is considered concentrated. A flame may appear to jump because the reference relation between source and target is rewritten.

Al-MerikaniAmanah

Fire is permitted under conditions. Seals specify what may burn, for how long, and what must remain untouched.

TemenggongCommunion

Calls remembered fire: kiln heat from old workshops, lightning from storm-scarred trees, hearth-heat from ancestral homes.

Lan FangRecomposition

The virtuoso tradition. Converts chemical, kinetic, electrical and thermal relations through low-resistance chains.

Nine RainReciprocity

Never creates fire in isolation. Heat is exchanged across a wider cycle; every blaze has a cooling elsewhere or a future debt.

KoraThird Voice

Uses fire as identity and testimony: a flame may reveal what an object has survived, or burn only what belongs to a named history.

SundCommons

Distributed ignition and shielding. A group can carry heat-load collectively, making civic-scale furnaces and protective firebreaks possible.

EVOLVED FORM

Lan Fang develops Cascade Fire: a spell that never 'creates fire' but composes six lawful transitions so quickly that observers experience a fireball.

POWER 02 · Force · Space · Matter

Telekinesis, Flight & Force

Classical repertoire: levitation, flight, force pushes, barriers, moving objects

Operations: Move · Bind · Exchange
TKIParallax

Changes positional reference. The object is not pushed; its relation to here/there or up/down is locally redescribed.

Al-MerikaniAmanah

Binds motion to permission: remain, approach, stop here. Their force-fields behave like laws.

TemenggongCommunion

Restores an object's relation to a place it knows. Stones return to old walls; boats pull toward remembered docks.

Lan FangRecomposition

Directly engineers force, inertia and momentum. Most outsiders call this the cleanest telekinesis.

Nine RainReciprocity

Moves by exchanging load with a larger system. A boulder rises because weight is redistributed into ground, water, wind or time.

KoraThird Voice

Invokes historical role. A bell swings because it is remembered as ringing; a sword returns to the hand that defined it.

SundCommons

Splits work across participants. Hundreds can lift a structure that no individual could move without fatal resistance.

EVOLVED FORM

Sund develops Civic Lift: buildings, bridges and ships can be moved by coordinated neighbourhood-scale casting.

POWER 03 · Life · Identity · Pattern

Healing, Disease & Regeneration

Classical repertoire: wound healing, disease cure, regeneration, vitality

Operations: Restore · Transform · Bind
TKIParallax

Diagnoses competing descriptions of the body and finds the one in which recovery is still reachable. Exceptional at ambiguous illness.

Al-MerikaniAmanah

Healing as covenant: body, healer and patient enter explicit obligations. Powerful but ethically formalised.

TemenggongCommunion

Restores belonging between body, self, kin and place. Strongest where illness is entangled with displacement or broken relation.

Lan FangRecomposition

Bio-magical engineering: tissue repair, metabolic control, prosthetic integration, accelerated regeneration.

Nine RainReciprocity

Treats illness as system imbalance. Healing one body may require changing diet, water, ecology, labour rhythm or season.

KoraThird Voice

Repairs identity and memory after trauma, possession, magical erasure or personality fracture.

SundCommons

Distributes repair burden through a care network. Group healing can stabilise many people at once but exposes hidden inequalities of care.

EVOLVED FORM

Temenggong develops Return-to-Place Medicine; Kora develops Name-Stitching for damaged identity; Sund develops Ward Healing for entire districts.

POWER 04 · Life · Pattern · Space

Plants, Animals & Ecological Magic

Classical repertoire: plant growth, animal speech, vines, swarms, ecological control

Operations: Perceive · Restore · Exchange
TKIParallax

Perceives species through multiple ecological frames; can temporarily translate sensory worlds between human and non-human actors.

Al-MerikaniAmanah

Creates protected ecological covenants: grazing rights, no-kill zones, migration corridors and species-specific sanctuary.

TemenggongCommunion

The great communion tradition. Reads old forest relations, animal routes, soil memory and ancestral cultivation.

Lan FangRecomposition

Recomposes growth programs, plant morphology and living materials; produces engineered magical crops and bio-structures.

Nine RainReciprocity

Supreme at ecosystems. Works through trophic networks, rainfall, seed cycles, pollination and feedback over seasons.

KoraThird Voice

Hears ecological absence: the bird no longer present, the missing insect rhythm, the silence where a species used to answer.

SundCommons

Commons-based stewardship magic. Shared maintenance creates resilient gardens, fisheries and urban ecologies.

EVOLVED FORM

Nine Rain develops Succession Casting: a spell whose intended result appears over decades through carefully staged ecological transitions.

POWER 05 · Mind · Information · Identity

Telepathy, Emotion & Dream

Classical repertoire: mind-reading, empathy, emotion control, dreamwalking

Operations: Perceive · Exchange · Bind
TKIParallax

Overlaps viewpoints. Telepathy is temporary parallax between observers.

Al-MerikaniAmanah

Mind-contact requires terms. Consent, witness and boundary are formalised; coercive enchantment is treated as a severe breach.

TemenggongCommunion

Reads mind through relation to place, kin, ritual and memory. Dreams often appear as landscapes.

Lan FangRecomposition

Maps attention, emotion and cognitive signal as manipulable systems. Powerful, precise, and ethically controversial.

Nine RainReciprocity

Treats emotion as a group feedback system. Can modulate panic, trust or collective attention through environmental conditions.

KoraThird Voice

Master tradition. Polyphonic telepathy preserves several voices without forcing agreement; memory and identity remain distinct channels.

SundCommons

Builds temporary cognitive commons: shared situational awareness, distributed attention and group decision support.

EVOLVED FORM

Kora develops Polyphony: simultaneous multi-mind perception without synthesis. Sund develops Shared Attention for rescue, war and civic coordination.

POWER 06 · Information · Mind · Space

Illusion, Invisibility & Glamour

Classical repertoire: invisibility, disguise, sensory illusion, hallucination

Operations: Transform · Perceive · Sever
TKIParallax

Allows two descriptions of the same scene to coexist. Their illusions are strongest when both readings are locally defensible.

Al-MerikaniAmanah

Controls who has permission to perceive what. Invisibility becomes access control.

TemenggongCommunion

Concealment through belonging: the hidden thing is made to count as part of the place, like a stone among stones.

Lan FangRecomposition

Engineers light, sound and sensory signal directly. Produces spectacular, reproducible projection systems.

Nine RainReciprocity

Changes expectation through context: weather, scent, movement, timing and attention conspire to make something effectively unseen.

KoraThird Voice

Edits narrative salience. An object can remain visible yet become difficult to remember as important.

SundCommons

Collective anonymity. Groups can obscure individual identity while preserving coordinated action.

EVOLVED FORM

Kora develops Narrative Blindness: the target is perceived but fails to enter the observer's meaningful account of events.

POWER 07 · Information · Pattern · Space

Clairvoyance, Detection & Divination

Classical repertoire: remote viewing, magic detection, prophecy, sensing danger

Operations: Perceive · Exchange
TKIParallax

Triangulation masters. Infers unknown states from differences among partial observations.

Al-MerikaniAmanah

Reads commitments, permissions and breaches. Excellent at detecting hidden contracts, curses and broken oaths.

TemenggongCommunion

Reads traces in place, object and lineage. A site can reveal what has repeatedly happened there.

Lan FangRecomposition

Instrumental divination: arrays, detectors, calibrated crystals and machine-assisted magical sensing.

Nine RainReciprocity

Forecasts trajectories. Best at weather, ecology, social cycles and threshold events.

KoraThird Voice

Detects missing information and contradictory accounts. Exceptional at erased history and concealed identity.

SundCommons

Distributed sensing: many weak observations combine into highly reliable collective situational awareness.

EVOLVED FORM

TKI develops Unknown-Variable Sight: seeing the structure of what information is missing.

POWER 08 · Space · Law · Pattern

Teleportation, Portals & Distance

Classical repertoire: teleportation, portals, folding space, pocket realms

Operations: Move · Bind · Transform
TKIParallax

Changes adjacency: two places become validly next to each other within a chosen frame.

Al-MerikaniAmanah

Travels along trusted routes. Old exchange corridors become low-resistance spatial contracts.

TemenggongCommunion

Uses remembered routes: pilgrimage paths, river crossings, footpaths, animal trails and ancestral roads.

Lan FangRecomposition

Engineers spatial topology directly; strongest at constructed gates, fixed portals and transport infrastructure.

Nine RainReciprocity

Moves through seasonal and network alignment. A route may exist only under the right celestial, ecological or calendrical conditions.

KoraThird Voice

Travels through named relation: the room where she waited, the field where the treaty was sung. Identity anchors destination.

SundCommons

Creates shared passage: communities can hold a corridor open through continuous collective maintenance.

EVOLVED FORM

Al-Merikani develops Route Sovereignty: the oldest ports become magically closer to one another than their geographic distance suggests.

POWER 09 · Life · Matter · Identity

Shapeshifting, Body & Form

Classical repertoire: shapeshifting, size change, body modification, disguise

Operations: Transform · Restore · Bind
TKIParallax

Shifts which bodily description is privileged: age, role, gendered presentation, animal analogy, social face.

Al-MerikaniAmanah

Form changes are bound by declared identity clauses. Excellent at controlled transformation without loss of self.

TemenggongCommunion

Transforms toward remembered kinship with animal, ancestor or landscape form; highly relational and ritualised.

Lan FangRecomposition

Most technically advanced morphology: muscle, bone, skin, organ, scale and material integration.

Nine RainReciprocity

Changes body in relation to environment: lungs for altitude, skin for drought, metabolism for season.

KoraThird Voice

Protects continuity of name and memory through transformation. Can identify a shapeshifter despite perfect physical disguise.

SundCommons

Group-form magic: temporary distributed bodies, shared sensory organs and collective locomotion.

EVOLVED FORM

Sund develops Plural Body: a coordinated group functions briefly as one distributed organism without erasing individual identity.

POWER 10 · Identity · Law · Information

Spirits, Summoning & Ancestors

Classical repertoire: summoning, banishing, ghosts, ancestor contact, possession

Operations: Bind · Perceive · Exchange
TKIParallax

Treats spirits as persistent relational models whose status depends on viewpoint and evidence. Skilled at distinguishing entity, echo and projection.

Al-MerikaniAmanah

Master of invitation, boundary and dismissal. Summoning is governed by terms, names, witnesses and permitted scope.

TemenggongCommunion

Relates spirits to place, graves, heirlooms, waterways and custodianship. Strong where memory and location are dense.

Lan FangRecomposition

Builds containers and interfaces for spirit-like persistence; controversial experiments blur spirit, construct and copied personality.

Nine RainReciprocity

Treats ancestral presence as part of ecological and temporal cycles; contact is seasonal and reciprocal.

KoraThird Voice

Master of lineage and remembered identity. Can call an account without claiming that the account is literally the whole person.

SundCommons

Collective remembrance can sustain civic ancestors, institutional personae and shared protective identities.

EVOLVED FORM

Kora develops Witness-Summoning: reconstructing the perspective of a dead or absent person from relational traces without claiming full resurrection.

POWER 11 · Law · Space · Information

Wards, Binding & Countermagic

Classical repertoire: magic shields, anti-magic, seals, prisons, dispelling

Operations: Bind · Sever · Exchange
TKIParallax

Destabilises hostile magic by exposing contradictions in its causal description.

Al-MerikaniAmanah

Supreme ward tradition: permissions, exclusions, conditions, witnesses, thresholds, expiry and jurisdiction.

TemenggongCommunion

A ward becomes strong when it is embedded in a maintained place: gate, grove, shrine, house, shoreline.

Lan FangRecomposition

Countermagic as reverse engineering. Identify operator sequence, then interrupt the weakest dependency.

Nine RainReciprocity

Does not block; redirects. Dangerous magic is absorbed, dispersed or returned into larger cycles.

KoraThird Voice

Attacks identity of the spell: who authored it, what story sustains it, what name keeps it coherent.

SundCommons

Collective shielding. A ward can be distributed across a population so no single breach collapses the whole field.

EVOLVED FORM

TKI develops Contradiction Countermagic: hostile spells fail because their hidden assumptions are made mutually incompatible.

Weather, Water & Atmosphere

Classical repertoire: rain, storms, wind, fog, drought, temperature

Operations: Exchange · Transform · Restore
TKIParallax

Perceives competing atmospheric models and exploits threshold uncertainty: small reframings at unstable moments produce large changes.

Al-MerikaniAmanah

Weather rites are covenants with territory, season and use; rain may be granted under conditions.

TemenggongCommunion

Calls weather remembered by place: monsoon routes, sea breezes, storm scars, river fog.

Lan FangRecomposition

Directly engineers pressure, heat and charge. Can make spectacular local weather, at high resistance cost.

Nine RainReciprocity

Supreme tradition. Works with water cycles, cloud nucleation, soil, plants, season and long-range feedback.

KoraThird Voice

Reads weather as historical voice: droughts and storms leave cultural memory that can become part of prediction and ritual.

SundCommons

Civic climate maintenance: heat relief, flood defence and wind management through shared infrastructure.

EVOLVED FORM

Nine Rain develops Weather Debt Accounting: every forced atmospheric change is logged against a regional future balance.

POWER 13 · Pattern · Information · Law

Probability, Timing & Time

Classical repertoire: luck, precognition, slowing, haste, time loops

Operations: Perceive · Transform · Bind
TKIParallax

Sees branching descriptions of possible near-futures and chooses a frame with favourable constraints.

Al-MerikaniAmanah

Binds deadlines, durations and expiry. Exceptional at until, after, for seven days, once only.

TemenggongCommunion

Uses historical recurrence: festivals, tides, migrations, generational cycles, remembered timings.

Lan FangRecomposition

Manipulates rates and clocks locally; can accelerate processes but pays steep resistance for deep temporal distortion.

Nine RainReciprocity

Masters timing. The right action at the right threshold can resemble probability control.

KoraThird Voice

Reads narrative inevitability and repetition: what history is trying to make happen again.

SundCommons

Synchronises groups. Shared tempo can make coordinated action appear impossibly fast or improbably fortunate.

EVOLVED FORM

Nine Rain develops Threshold Luck: waiting until a system is one perturbation away from the desired outcome.

POWER 14 · Mind · Identity · Information

Memory, Identity & Erasure

Classical repertoire: memory reading, forgetting, identity alteration, psychometry

Operations: Perceive · Restore · Sever
TKIParallax

Compares multiple self-descriptions and can reveal where identity changes across contexts.

Al-MerikaniAmanah

Treats identity as witnessed continuity. Names, records and vows anchor a person against erasure.

TemenggongCommunion

Memory distributed across place and object can restore personal recollection.

Lan FangRecomposition

Can edit memory precisely, but technical success does not answer the ethical question of who remains afterward.

Nine RainReciprocity

Memory is cyclical and environmental: habit, season, place and community can reconstitute what an individual lost.

KoraThird Voice

Supreme tradition. Reads missing voices, repairs narrative continuity and distinguishes memory from identity.

SundCommons

Externalises memory into community practice. A person may forget, while the group continues to carry the pattern.

EVOLVED FORM

Kora develops Absence Forensics: detecting the contour of something erased by the distortions its absence leaves behind.

POWER 15 · Life · Identity · Pattern · Law

Death, Resurrection & Persistence

Classical repertoire: resurrection, undeath, life extension, soul return

Operations: Restore · Bind · Exchange
TKIParallax

Asks which description of the person remains recoverable. Philosophically powerful, practically cautious.

Al-MerikaniAmanah

Requires explicit terms of return: whose identity, what obligations survive, who witnesses continuity.

TemenggongCommunion

Strongest where body, grave, kin, objects and place preserve dense relational continuity.

Lan FangRecomposition

Can rebuild body and neural pattern, but cannot by engineering alone settle whether the restored person is the same person.

Nine RainReciprocity

Rejects isolated resurrection as ecologically impossible; any return requires exchange with wider cycles of life and death.

KoraThird Voice

Can reconstruct voice, identity and memory traces with unmatched subtlety, yet is strict about the difference between witness and person.

SundCommons

A community may collectively sustain fragments of a person long after death; resurrection becomes a question of distributed continuity.

EVOLVED FORM

The Schools disagree whether true resurrection is possible at all. Their disagreement becomes one of the great philosophical and legal crises of the world.
