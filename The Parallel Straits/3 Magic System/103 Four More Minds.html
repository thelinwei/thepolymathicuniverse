01 · Begin with what becomes possible

Four more routes.

Lan Fang, Sané-Kora, Nine Rain and Sri Temenggong each send a mind. Two of them work at Gift 5. One works at Gift 4 and lets stone and rain carry the rest. One works at Gift 3 and lets a thousand hands carry the rest.

The sheets are mature profiles: Book Two’s eight attributes on a 1–5 veteran scale, with Gift, capacities, rank and derived tracks as the Ledger defines them. Every card stands in one of three places: Established, taught and proven; Stretch, working and still being worked out in the houses; Frontier, seen and still unexplained.

09 · Lan Fang · School of Medicine / Mozi Yard

华鹤 · Hua He

after 华佗 Hua Tuo

He knows where the body opens, and how to close it again.

Morning in the apothecary yard: forty students move as tiger, deer, bear, ape and crane. Inside, a dockhand sleeps through the surgery on his own arm, smiling. Hua He washes his needles and asks who is next.

Where the genius lives

Deep Gift 5 in the living body: its channels, its points, its recoveries. Sense 5 reads a body like a map; Coordination 5 puts a needle exactly where the map says. The same knowledge heals, sleeps, stills or kills.

Lan Fang’s physicians look like the gentlest people on a battlefield, and they are the most precise. Hua He teaches both halves of the art to the same students, in the same order: first the healing, then the rest.
