Outcome first: the Ledger holds nearly everything the twelve minds asked of it. Where they pressed hardest, the rules open further toward wonder, and the four questions still open become part of the world.

01 · Settle now

Nine rulings.

These close the gaps the first eight minds found, so every card in both laboratories has one reading.

A1 · Sharing

Sharing divides one total.

Each member pays the total divided by the participants, rounded down; the leader pays the remainder. Combining power pools dice. Only an axiom brings efficiency. This follows 90; 85’s prose, worked sample and ensemble appendix take the same wording.

A2 · Three measures

Extent, complexity, novelty.

Extent sets the Exertion cost: Small 1, Real 2, Bold 4, Grand 8, Mighty 16. Complexity, from 1 to 5, counts the relations that must hold at once; it sets the Sense needed to compose the spell and the Operation rating needed to cast it from the songbook. Novelty sets the path: a known recipe casts, a variant takes one trial, a new composition takes three, a new axiom is a frontier. A tiny spell can be a work of genius.

A3 · Harm

Harm follows the size ladder.

A harming spell deals its size in Health, spread across named targets as the caster declares: Small 1, Real 2, Bold 4, Grand 8, Mighty 16. Structures and machines take harm the same way. Restraint deals none until a recipe writes a wounding cadence, and many restraints have one.

A4 · Beyond Mighty

The ladder keeps doubling.

Legendary 32 Exertion and 10 World dice; Mythic 64 and 15; World-making 128 and 20. National workings sit at Mythic. These are 85’s figures, adopted until a national formula is written.

A5 · Handover

A working outlives its hands.

A sustained working survives a change of participants when outgoing and incoming overlap for one round. It is priced once, for its declared duration.

A6 · Keys at legendary size

Bare hands to Mighty; a legend above it.

Every spell up to Mighty casts bare-handed. From Legendary up, the key is a legendary instrument or a legendary place, and either one lifts the caster one rank for that working. One lift per working.

A7 · Affinity

Affinity is dice.

+1 die each for house, faculty and specialism, up to +3, added to the casting pool.

A8 · A record that acts

Four marks of a valid record.

A Parallax record acts when it names its target, states an identifiable transformation, shows its assumptions, and carries a clean release. Jurisdiction is the ground the target stands on.

A9 · Opposing a crowd

A crowd resists as a ward.

Its strongest member’s Resolve, plus one die for every tenfold of people: 10, 100, 1,000. A charge of a thousand riders resists with its best rider’s Resolve + 3.

02 · Loosen

Six rules open further.

The four new minds were chosen to press where the eight had barely gone: the body, the spoken word, the weather and the crowd. Each pressed one rule open. The six that follow are adopted, and every new card is written to them.

L1 · Precision

Size prices the reach; precision prices the wound.

A spell aimed at a vulnerability its caster has first read with Perceive may concentrate harm beyond its size, up to the target’s full Health. A Real-sized touch can end a life. The opposed roll still stands.

Asked for by The Pressed Point.

L2 · Many hands

Small castings add up.

Castings woven to one plan sum to the size of their combined extent. The Weaver holds the Thread at the size of the largest single share, and the sum rolls its World dice once. Gift 0 planters join at cantrip cost. This is how a Gift 3 caster builds a Mighty ridge and a Mythic country.

Asked for by The Green Belt.

L3 · Living renewal

A tradition can renew a working.

A working whose Thread is a performed tradition is renewed each time someone who learned it performs it, at 1 Exertion a telling. It rests when the last teller falls silent. Any house may compose one; Sané-Kora composes them best.

Asked for by The Unforgotten.

L4 · The key

A true secret opens any wall.

A true secret, a named taboo or a read vulnerability strips rank and ward dice from the defender, who resists with Resolve alone. Underdogs can win; great casters guard their secrets.

Asked for by The Sorcerer’s Secret.

L5 · The world pays

Built forms can feed themselves.

A working built into form whose expendables arrive on their own, rain, sun, tide, wind or breath, renews itself while they keep arriving. The first casting pays the full price for its size; the weather pays the upkeep. When the supply stops, the working rests.

Asked for by The Mountain That Keeps the Rain.

L6 · Practice

Training raises the ceiling.

Daily practice of a joined form adds one Exertion box per decade, up to three. Capacity grows the way an athlete’s does.

Asked for by The Five Animals Play.

What stays firm

The doubling cost ladder, Gift 0–5, the eight Operations, the seven axioms and dice at scale. Loosening reaches the edges of the system; its spine carries the extra weight.

03 · Keep open as world

Four frontiers.

These live in the story. Each has been seen once. Mechanically, a frontier working is cast by the mind who first did it, at the ladder price for its size, and enters the songbook when a second caster repeats it.

  • Can a spell arrive already perfected? The Open Page
  • Is there an eighth axiom? The Curved Approach
  • Can a made thing become a caster? Neumark’s workshop heir
  • Can reasoning found a world? I Have a World
04 · Housekeeping

For the next edit.

Names. The twelve are a.m.-era minds with Straits names, each noted after the historical figure who inspired them. Card codes keep their first letters, so references stay stable.

Standing. Cards carry Established, Stretch or Frontier, a measure of how known a working is in the houses.

Volume 85. Takes A1’s sharing wording and A4’s ladder into its prose, sample and appendix.

Files. The first folio, saved as Three Minds Beyond the Syllabus, now lives as Eight Minds at the Edge of the Rules.
