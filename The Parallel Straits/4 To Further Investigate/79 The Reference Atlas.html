[The Formula]

(Root × stream × contemporary hand × material move.)

XV
Thread XV

Root × stream × hand × move

Four worked combinations.

Each design in the Straits can be built from four parts: a root practice, a reference from one of the house’s secondary streams, a contemporary hand, and a material move. The stream keeps the design honest to the roads. The contemporary hand keeps it buildable. The material move makes it new.

Al-MerikaniJantar Mantar×Swahili carved door×Al Bahr’s moving screenAn observatory gate whose screen opens at the hour a contract falls due.
Lan FangTulou ring×Pua kumbu×Wang Shu’s salvaged wallsA round assembly hall whose inner wall is rewoven by the Dayak families at every vote.
Nine RainQ’eswachaka×Piña cloth×Knit formworkA footbridge rewoven each year, with a translucent rain veil that becomes the next year’s concrete mould.
Sané-KoraToguna×Lamba silk×Kéré’s floating roofA low speaking roof with a silk ceiling that lifts as the argument cools.

Run any material move from Part Two through this formula with a different house, and the result will belong to that house. That is how the unhoused ideas find homes.
