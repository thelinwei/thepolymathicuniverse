THE OUTCOME FIRST

Five mutations worth
making before we decide.

These are not designs to adopt. They are provocations with enough cultural and material logic to generate a genuinely new architectural language.

01

The school as an instrument

Al-Merikani’s masonry, copper and cloth form inhabitable astronomical machinery—not a decorative observatory.

02

Fabric becomes geometry

A knitted or reefable membrane stops being a roof covering and becomes formwork, shade logic, or environmental data.

03

Rain makes the plan

Nine Rain’s architecture is an ecology of catchment, storage and seasons—not a Maya-shaped object.

04

The school is performed

Sané-Kora develops from memory, acoustics and public acts of repair into spaces that change when people gather.

05

Everything has a second life

Sund’s shipyard, Lan Fang’s joints and Toa Keng’s civic courts transform salvage into new institutional rituals.

∞

Nothing owns a material

The same membrane can be a sail at Sri, a measuring veil at Al-Merikani, or an archive wall at Kora.

Twelve speculative systems to prototype

EXPERIMENT 01 · fabric → climate interface

Monsoon veil

Reefable high-tensile woven panels with controllable porosity; collect droplets in stitched channels.

POTENTIAL HOMESSri / Nine Rain / Al-Merikani
TEST FIRSTWind uplift, cleaning, UV degradation; avoid claiming waterproofness without tests.
EXPERIMENT 02 · knit → formwork → shell

The knitted stone

Program fibre density, tension a prefabricated sleeve, then spray mineral coating or cast lightweight earth/concrete.

POTENTIAL HOMESSund / Lan Fang / Nine Rain
TEST FIRSTCompression geometry, shrinkage, demoulding and fire rating.
EXPERIMENT 03 · brick → instrument

Rain register masonry

Long custom brick modules create variable-density, pixelated screens; moisture and sun exposures determine patterns.

POTENTIAL HOMESNine Rain / Toa Keng
TEST FIRSTHorizontal spans, seismic/wind action and draining mortar joints.
EXPERIMENT 04 · metal + sunlight → time

Copper-shadow astrolabe

A horizontal field of adjustable metal baffles, calibration marks and movable veils inscribes seasonal paths.

POTENTIAL HOMESAl-Merikani / Nine Rain
TEST FIRSTGlare, thermal expansion, public safety and long-term calibration.
EXPERIMENT 05 · cloth + clay → sound

Listening membrane

Double-layer acoustic textile hangs below segmented clay vaults; motion changes the room’s reverberation.

POTENTIAL HOMESSané-Kora / Al-Merikani
TEST FIRSTAcoustic simulation, flammability, fibre dust, accessible maintenance.
EXPERIMENT 06 · timber node → roof ecology

Joint that grows

Reversible multi-axis wooden connector grows into branching structures that accept local roof panels and tree apertures.

POTENTIAL HOMESLan Fang / Sri Temenggong
TEST FIRSTLoad path, fabrication tolerances, decay and tree growth clearance.
EXPERIMENT 07 · salvage metal → flexible skin

The repair tapestry

Thousands of re-used metal scales clipped to mesh make swaying screens that reveal cumulative repairs.

POTENTIAL HOMESSund / Sané-Kora
TEST FIRSTSharp edges, falling objects, corrosion, noise in wind.
EXPERIMENT 08 · sail logic → movable public space

Seasonal courtyard tent

An assembly court changes from hard civic hall to soft shaded gathering place with manually reefed canopies.

POTENTIAL HOMESToa Keng / Sri Temenggong
TEST FIRSTStorm lockout and operation plan, moving parts, anchorage.
EXPERIMENT 09 · biopolymer → temporary membrane

Biological weathering skin

Cellulose/chitosan skins with graded translucency and planned decomposition as ritual replacement surfaces.

POTENTIAL HOMESNine Rain / Sund
TEST FIRSTMostly speculative outdoors: humidity, fungi, structural strength, environmental claims.
EXPERIMENT 10 · perforation → shadow script

Light that carries text

Layered etched panels turn movement into changing projected inscriptions, not fixed ornament.

POTENTIAL HOMESAl-Merikani / Sané-Kora
TEST FIRSTLegibility, glare and cultural rights to patterns and scripts.
EXPERIMENT 11 · buoyancy + sails → learning

The floating archive

A moored, modular water classroom deploys wind screens, inspection decks and removable hull modules.

POTENTIAL HOMESSri Temenggong / Sund
TEST FIRSTMarine codes, life safety, storms, corrosion and flood design.
EXPERIMENT 12 · woven fibres + porous mineral

Thick air / thin wall

Gradient-porosity mineral textile panels catch dust and modulate airflow, with visible repair layers.

POTENTIAL HOMESSané-Kora / Nine Rain / Lan Fang
TEST FIRSTFiltering performance, cleaning, pressure loss, safety of fibres.
HOW TO USE THE ATLAS

Transfer verbs,
not appearances.

ObserveWhat practical, spiritual, political or technical problem did the original object solve?
MutateWhat happens if that mechanism is magnified, inverted, recombined, digitised or made mobile?
TestWhat must change because of climate, force, cost, labour, cultural meaning and long-term maintenance?

Historical specificity

Ulugh Beg and Jai Singh do not belong to one homogeneous astronomical tradition. Maya and Inka precedents are not a single American culture. Mandé music and Nigerien civic architecture have distinct genealogies. Sámi traditions are not interchangeable with Scandinavian modernism.

Novelty must be structural

A new silhouette is not enough. Try changing the relationship between instrument and building, fabric and load, moisture and masonry, student and institution, or one generation’s building and the next generation’s repair.

Make, then investigate

Prototype with paper, cloth, string, clay, brick and simple digital geometry. Trace forces, daylight, acoustic reflections, drainage and repair cycles. The things that fail may be more interesting than the things that look immediately polished.
