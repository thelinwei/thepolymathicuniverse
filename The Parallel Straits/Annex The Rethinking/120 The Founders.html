The Polymathic Universe · Book Two · The founders of magic
[The Founders]天漏 · 开山 · 绝学
(the ten years when the sky began to leak, the Gift 6 minds who could already work through it, the seats they held in the seven houses, and the legendary workings they left behind)

Before anyone could cast, a few people already could.

Claude4 November 2026 · worldbuilding · canon draft from Linwei’s brief

The outcomeThe founders in six lines

  1. 天漏, the Loosening (1908–1918). After the Evenki Flash, the World’s resistance thinned toward the threshold of magic without crossing it. Historians named those ten years only decades later.
  2. Gift 6. The Gift that worked during the Loosening. A handful of minds could coax workings through the gap by instinct, ten years before anyone else could cast. Attributes now run from 1 to 10, with the founders’ signature attributes at 9 and 10.
  3. 时势造英雄，英雄造时势. The Loosening made the founders. Their meeting at the Monsoon Convocation of 1918 tipped the world over the threshold, and the Kindling happened in the room where they sat.
  4. The seats. In 1918 the founders already headed the seven houses, under each house’s own title: Rector at Sund, Headmaster at Toa Keng, Kumatigi at Sané-Kora, 山长 at Lan Fang, Nāẓir at Al-Merikani, Amauta at Nine Rain, Guru at Sri Temenggong.
  5. Sixteen founders. Twelve from the earlier character work, and four proposed to fill the thinnest houses. Every founder carries both names: the one the story uses, and the real figure behind it.
  6. 绝学. A fourth spell standing, Legendary. The founders’ grand workings are fully recorded, and wait for a rare Gifted caster to reproduce or reimagine them. Five were cast at the Veyr Retaliation. What their students inherited was each founder’s method.

Before the Kindling 天漏

Ten years in which magic was almost possible, and only the founders noticed.

I
1908 to 1918

The Loosening 天漏

Of axiomatic laws and resistance, and the decade the sky began to leak.

The Evenki Flash of 1908 lit the sky over the northern forest, and in the years that followed, the World’s resistance to magic began to thin. Most people noticed nothing, or shrugged off the odd moment: a lamp that stayed lit, a seed that sprouted in a drawer, a drum that lost its sound for one heartbeat. A few people felt the gap clearly and learned to coax workings through it, each in their own idiom.

Those few already held seats in the seven houses of the Straits. They worked out the rules by instinct, years before the rules could be written, and some of what they made in those years has never been repeated.

II
The scale widens

Gift 6, and a scale to 10

The ones who pierced the veil were not ordinary people, by circumstance or by providence.

Gift 6 is the Gift that worked during the Loosening. Before 1918 the World’s resistance sat above everyone else’s reach. Gift 6 could get through 天漏. When the Kindling lowered the threshold, Gift 0 to 5 opened for everyone, and Gift 6 stayed what it had been: rare.

Part Four

What they left 绝学

The spells mostly stayed with the founders. Their methods went to everyone.

V
A fourth standing

Legendary workings 绝学

Documented, and waiting for someone who can cast them.

EstablishedTaught and cast across the houses.
StretchCast by masters at the edge of their rank.
FrontierNew, proven by few, still being refined.
Legendary · 绝学Fully recorded. Needs Gift 6, or a reimagining.

A 绝学 is a founder’s grand working. Its recipe is written down in full, often in the founder’s own hand, and anyone may read it. Almost no one can cast it. The houses keep the records open and wait for a rare Gifted caster who can reproduce one, or find their own road to the same effect.

What passed down is the method behind each one: Gonxhe’s commons, Konggu’s opening and closing, Beek’s counterpoint, Mama Miti’s many hands. Students of every generation learn these methods, and the ordinary spells of the houses grow from them.

At the Veyr Retaliation five founders cast together. Tesslund silenced Veyr’s coupled systems, Konggu closed its war council, Beek held its army between two beats, Hua He put its garrisons to sleep, and Guna Watu let the stone of its fortresses go. The show of force that set a new magical world order was five 绝学 at once.
