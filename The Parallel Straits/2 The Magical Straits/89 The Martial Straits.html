THE PROVOCATION

Not seven answers to the same question.
Seven different arguments about what a fighter is.

In 金庸’s 笑傲江湖, 华山派’s 气宗 / 剑宗 conflict turns a training emphasis into a schism. For PMU, the inspiring device is the disagreement: different traditions can become genuinely brilliant by perfecting different premises.

These schools do not have seven exclusive elements, and every one can learn armed and unarmed magic. Their history, trades and curricula make some techniques easier to invent and more elegant to perform. Here are two possible martial lineages within each school, then the outrageous form they might invent together.

Body

Footwork, breath, weight, rhythm, grappling and empty-hand techniques. A body can be a research instrument.

Instrument

Keris, sword, paddle, staff, chain, bow, survey rod or the architecture of the room itself.

World

The advanced fighter recruits tides, streets, stages, shipyards and entire ensembles as part of the encounter.

The tags such as “Force · Exchange” identify possible Domain × Operation readings. They are interpretive notes, not final mechanical constraints. Spells have no fixed Exertion costs yet.

✦ GRAND SYNTHESIS / Lan Fang ✦

百榫归锋 · The Returning Blades

The beams and loose joinery of an unfinished academy rise into a thousand illuminated wooden-and-metal blades. They sail as a flock, form shelters for fleeing students, then return as walkways and roofs. The physician school controls the living rhythm of the formation; the joiners control its changing geometry.

A flying sword art that can rebuild what it disassembles.

WHEN TRADITIONS MEET

Three duels that invent a third art

The most interesting martial artist may be a student trained in two incompatible styles—or two rivals who accidentally make something together.

01
Sund × Lan Fang

The Glowing Sleeve

A Sund quench-fighter catches a Lan Fang segmented blade on a steel glove. The sword bends around the glove; the glove sheds its heat into the blade’s hinges. For one instant the weapon becomes a tiny forge. Neither school’s usual counter works.

A new discipline appears: thermal joinery fighting.

02
Kora × Sri Temmengong

The Silence under the Stage

A Kora circle omits a beat to hide the wrestler’s advance. A Sri shadow-dancer steps beneath the platform and borrows the missing beat as a moving shadow. The drum returns—and seems to strike two places at once.

A rhythm can become a place; a place can become an instrument.

03
Nine Rains × Al Merikani

The Arrow that Returns as Rain

An Al Merikani archer sets an exact silver arc. A Nine Rains fighter changes the current inside the arrow’s trail, drawing a loop of water behind it. The returned arrow lands dry, and the falling water protects its intended target.

A trajectory becomes a cycle; an attack becomes a shelter.
