[The Seven Trades at Play]七行之戏
(forty-eight spells from seven houses, each with the job it was made for, the second life people found for it, who took it up first, where it came from, and where its burden lands)

A rivet glows in a boy’s hands and frost forms at his feet. Within a week the apprentices are throwing snowballs at the foreman.

Claude1 November 2026 · rethinks The Seven Trades and Fire and Medicine

The outcome48 spells, eight duets, one cup

Every spell starts with what a witness would see, then gives its second life, its first adopters, its ancestry, and where the burden goes.

  1. Axioms are generative. Each one decides what new magic its house alone can invent.
  2. Second life is a rule. Misusing a spell cleverly is how variants are born, and every variant records its parent.
  3. Harm is real. Furnace Breath blisters, Flash Flood drowns, Nine Refusals drops men from towers.
  4. The burden is scenery. Frost at a boy’s feet, a harbour full of boiled crabs, a judge who ages a year per ruling.
  5. The joining maths is fixed, affinity comes from house, faculty and person, and each house sets its own standard of proof.

I
Thread I

The seven axioms

Each axiom decides what new magic its house alone can invent.

An axiom is a way of acting on the world. It decides which new recipes a house can bring through its trials, and mastery of it makes the house’s own magic cheaper. Anyone can learn a finished recipe from a teacher.

Sund · The Commons 共担

Sund makes many separate things into one body: hands into a ring, a crew into its hull, a street into a bucket chain.

Only this house can invent
Recipes in which separate systems become one system.
Mastery
A Sund ring of six or more pays one rung less for anything it casts together.
Proven when
A whole crew has used it for a season, through at least one storm.
Forge Hall 锻造馆 · Keel Yard 龙骨场 · Rigging Loft 索具楼 · Fire Watch 火巡队 · Common Table 公桌堂

Lan Fang · Recomposition 重组

Lan Fang takes the parts of a thing and sets them into a new whole: a fever into sweat and sleep, a broken loom into a better one, a siege tower into a pile of planks.

Only this house can invent
Recipes that turn one arrangement into another from the same parts.
Mastery
A master of three affinity pays one rung less for any recomposition.
Proven when
A hundred patients or a hundred chairs. For the Mozi College, one gate that held.
College of Medicine 医学院 · Joinery Academy 榫卯学堂 · Dye and Weave Works 染织坊 · Kiln House 窑火所 · Mozi College 墨子学院

Toa Keng · Parallax 换框

Toa Keng changes the frame. From another standpoint a thing is true in another way, and a record, a map or a ruling can act on the world.

Only this house can invent
Recipes in which a record, a map or a judgement changes what it describes.
Mastery
A master pays one rung less for any working that rests on a written record.
Proven when
Fifty documented trials, published in the Gazette with every witness named.
School of Law 法学院 · Surveyors’ Hall 测绘堂 · The Press 报馆 · Counting House 算馆 · The European Rooms 欧洲馆

Al-Merikani · Amanah 托付

Al-Merikani holds a thing in trust and releases it on time: warmth, a break, a debt, a flood.

Only this house can invent
Recipes that carry something through time intact.
Mastery
A master pays one rung less for any working released on a named day.
Proven when
Seven witnessed casts, each witness named, and each release on the very day promised.
The Observatory 观星台 · House of Numbers 算学院 · Instrument Makers 仪器坊 · The Trust House 托付馆 · The Garden Court 园庭

Sri Temenggong · Communion 相问

Sri Temenggong asks first, and works with the answer. A tree, a river mouth, a slope or a body can agree.

Only this house can invent
Recipes that are agreements with a place.
Mastery
A working the place has agreed to three times costs one rung less in that place.
Proven when
The same working in the same place for three monsoons, with the place still willing.
The Coast School 海岸学堂 · The Dusun 果园 · The Loom House 织锦坊 · House of the Body 身体馆 · The Land Court 土地庭

Nine Rain · Reciprocity 循环

Nine Rain turns what one working puts out into what the next takes in. Borrow, return, and return again.

Only this house can invent
Recipes that are cycles.
Mastery
A cycle that has run a full year costs one rung less on every later turn.
Proven when
One full seasonal cycle, with the debt returned.
The Chinampa Works 浮田坊 · The Terrace School 梯田学堂 · The Cord House 结绳馆 · The Calendar Court 历法庭 · The Loom 织堂

Sané-Kora · The Third Voice 第三声

Sané-Kora puts two different things together and draws out a third that neither held: two voices, two books, two metals, two people.

Only this house can invent
Recipes that need two unlike things to meet.
Mastery
A working cast as a true duet costs one rung less.
Proven when
A song remembered across three generations, or a manuscript copied by three hands.
The Kora Hall 科拉堂 · The Manuscript House 手稿馆 · The Smiths’ Society 铁匠会 · The Healers’ Yard 疗院 · The Hunters’ Society 猎人会

Thread II · 9 spells

Sund 松德

A shipwrighting people. Their magic came out of the forge, the slipway and the rigging, and it is always better with more hands.

Minute · 0Sund · Forge Hall

Pocket Winter 袖中冬

Cup a cold rivet in both hands and it glows cherry-red, ready to drive. The heat comes out of the air around you, so for a moment there is frost on the planks at your feet.

Second life

Apprentices run it backwards in July: the rivet stays cold, the warmth goes into the foreman’s tea, and a snowball’s worth of rime gathers in their palms. Snowball ambushes in the yard are now a Sund tradition, held in the hottest week of the year.

First adopted byRiveting gangs at the Keel Yard (a.m. 6); then ice-cream carts on the harbour front (a.m. 19), who never looked back.
AncestryGrew from Warm Rivet. Grew into Cold Room and Frost Line.
Where the burden goesThe air close by. In a crowded tram, everyone near you shivers and knows exactly what you did.
Real · 2, sharedSund · Common Table

Everyone’s Shoulder 众肩

A ring shares a weight so exactly that eight children carry a piano up four flights, each feeling a full schoolbag and no more.

Second life

Carrying a friend home from a wedding ‘as eight’, with seven friends who were not at the wedding. Removal crews charge by the shoulder.

First adopted byFuneral bearers and removalists (a.m. 9); then wedding processions, who carry the couple’s whole household through the streets.
AncestryGrew from the old yard chant for stepping a mast. Grew into Spread the Blow and every Sund ring since.
Where the burden goesShared to the gram: everyone’s legs ache the same amount next morning, which Sund regards as the whole point.
Small · 1Sund · Rigging Loft

Taut Line 紧索

A rope remembers its tension. A sail holds trimmed with no hand on the sheet; a hammock between two palms stays level all night.

Second life

Dormitory boys string a line between two windows across the lane and send notes, tea and once a cat. Festival tightrope walkers swear they never use it.

First adopted bySail lofts (a.m. 7); then laundresses, whose lines never sag; then the circus.
AncestryGrew from the rigger’s splice. Grew into Spider Bridge, a rope bridge strung across a gorge by twelve.
Where the burden goesThe rope itself. Every working ages it a month, and a line used all season frays by the monsoon.
Grand · 8 → 4 in a Sund ringSund · Keel Yard

Spread the Blow 散力

One blow is spread across a whole hull, so a storm sea or a ramming bow cracks no single plank. The rigging sings as the force runs up the masts.

Second life

Sund grandmothers cast a small one on staircases: a child who tumbles down feels nothing, and the stairs creak for a week. Fairground boxers have been caught spreading a punch across the whole crowd.

First adopted byTyphoon crews (a.m. 8); and the staircase grandmothers, who say they did it first.
AncestryGrew from Everyone’s Shoulder. Grew into The Whole Ship.
Where the burden goesThe crew feel the blow as one thump in the chest, together, like a drum heard through a wall.
Grand · 8 → 4 in a Sund ringSund · Fire Watch

Starve the Fire 饿火

A burning warehouse goes dark in a breath as its heat pours down a line of joined hands into the harbour.

Second life

Boiled Crab Night: every year the Fire Watch practise on a beach bonfire and cook the crabs in the shallows. It began as a scandal in a.m. 12, a harbour full of cooked fish, and became a festival.

First adopted byThe harbour Fire Watch (a.m. 10); then bakers, who learned to dump an oven’s heat in a second.
AncestryGrew from Pocket Winter, run at scale. Grew into Frost Line.
Where the burden goesThe harbour, which steams for an hour. Overdone, it poaches the shallows.
Real · 2Sund · Forge Hall

Furnace Breath 炉息

For one breath the air in front of the caster is forge-hot. Sails catch, glass cracks, skin blisters.

Second life

A Sund cook who sears a whole tuna in one breath has a queue round the block. The fire-breathers of the night market are unlicensed and very popular.

First adopted byBoarding defence on Sund merchantmen (a.m. 11); then, regrettably, kitchens.
AncestryGrew from the smith’s habit of blowing on coals. Grew into Slag Rain.
Where the burden goesThe caster’s own throat, raw for a day. Registered under the Concordat as a wounding working.
Wounds · Burns: 2 Health to anyone in the breath.
Grand · 8, sharedSund · Forge Hall

Slag Rain 渣雨

Every piece of iron an enemy carries runs hot and drips. Boarders drop their own cutlasses in a shower of sparks. Bronze shrugs it off, as Sund learned the hard way.

Second life

Scrap merchants melt a hulk’s worth of nails into one ingot in an afternoon. The rivet thief who tried it on the ship he was standing on is a song now.

First adopted byHarbour defence (a.m. 13); then the scrap yards.
AncestryGrew from Furnace Breath and the founder’s pour. Healed by Slag Garden.
Where the burden goesThe ring’s pooled heat, and the deck: scarred black with slag that no paint covers for a year.
Wounds · 3 Health to anyone holding iron.
Mighty · 16 → 8 in a Sund ringSund · Keel Yard

The Whole Ship 一船一身

A crew is coupled to its hull. Every hand feels the ship as their own body, and the ship steers itself through a typhoon on their reflexes.

Second life

The Sund rowing club won eleven regattas in a row before it was banned. Couples who try it on a sampan either never quarrel again or never stop.

First adopted byThe Norden storm fleets (a.m. 15); then the rowing club; then everyone who saw the rowing club.
AncestryGrew from Spread the Blow and Everyone’s Shoulder. Its children, Whole Street and Whole Village, are still argued over.
Where the burden goesEvery blow the hull takes, felt as bruises. Some old sailors carry a typhoon on their ribs for life.
Mighty · 16, sharedSund · Keel Yard

Keel-Breaker 断龙骨

Forty aboard one ship turn an enemy hull’s load path inward, and it breaks its own back at the keel.

Second life

None. Sund keeps looking, half hoping not to find one.

First adopted byLicensed by the Concord for the defence of a harbour mouth, and for nothing else (a.m. 14).
AncestryGrew from Spread the Blow, run backwards.
Where the burden goesThe ring, heavily; and the sea lane, which the fish abandon for a season.
Wounds · Ships, and whoever cannot swim.

Thread III · 9 spells

Lan Fang 兰芳

A making people, and a medical one. Their magic takes things apart and puts them back better, and the Mozi College keeps the city’s gates.

Minute · 0Lan Fang · College of Medicine

Second Steep 二泡

Spent tea leaves give a full, fresh second infusion, as good as the first.

Second life

One packet of good oolong stretched across a whole exam week. Certain tea houses ban it; aunties cast it on stingy hosts, out loud.

First adopted byDormitory kitchens (a.m. 5); then night-market tea stalls, which deny it.
AncestryGrew from the herbalist’s second decoction. Grew into Second Pot (soup) and, through a Kiln House student, Second Firing.
Where the burden goesThe leaves, which crumble to dust after. Cheap tea, stretched, is cheap tea twice.
Real · 2 → 1Lan Fang · College of Medicine

Sweat It Out 发汗

A fever leaves the body as a drenching sweat within the hour, and the patient sleeps.

Second life

Students who stayed up all night cast a gentle version to look properly ill on exam mornings. The College has a test for it, and a list.

First adopted byShophouse clinics during the fever season of a.m. 1, in secret; then every household medicine chest.
AncestryGrew from the classical fever formula. Grew into Furnace Formula with Sund, and into Fever Bloom.
Where the burden goesThe bedding. Fever linens are boiled, never reused, and on a hot monsoon night the heat needs cooling herbs to go anywhere at all.
Real · 2 → 1Lan Fang · Joinery Academy

Flat-Pack 拆件

Anything joined falls into its labelled parts: a chair, a loom, a crossbow, a wardrobe that will not fit through the door.

Second life

Removalists love it. Every year Joinery students reassemble the dean’s chair a little wrong, and every year the dean sits in it anyway. In a fight, a whole rack of weapons comes apart at once.

First adopted byRemoval crews (a.m. 8); then guardrooms; then the Joinery Academy’s pranksters, who were there all along.
AncestryGrew from Mortise Mend, run backwards. Grew into Nine Refusals.
Where the burden goesThe caster’s fingertips, numb for an hour. Forged one-piece things do not answer.
Real · 2Lan Fang · Kiln House

Staple Mend 锔瓷

A cracked porcelain bowl is mended with tiny staples of its own glaze, stronger than before and beautiful along the line.

Second life

Lucky bowls: at Lan Fang weddings a bowl is broken on purpose and mended at the table, so the marriage starts with a scar that holds. Antique forgers break new bowls to age them.

First adopted byKiln House menders (a.m. 6); then wedding families; then the forgers.
AncestryGrew from the street mender’s drill-and-staple craft. Grew into Self-Mending Rivet with Sund.
Where the burden goesA grain of the bowl’s weight per staple. A bowl mended three times rings a little lower.
Real · 2 → 1Lan Fang · Dye and Weave Works

Colour Lift 提色

The colour leaves a cloth and gathers in a basin of water, ready to be poured onto new cloth.

Second life

Children lift last year’s school shirt back to white for the new term. Lovers hide letters in the colour of a sarong. A wedding dress lifted to white is a mourning dress in some streets and a fresh start in others.

First adopted byThe Dye Works (a.m. 7); then laundries; then every mother before the school year.
AncestryGrew from the mordant bath. Grew into the dye river, where the Works pour colour back to the water on market days.
Where the burden goesThe cloth, a little thinner each time. After seven lifts, it tears.
Bold · 4 → 2Lan Fang · Mozi College

The Belt Wall 解带为城

Lay your belt on a table as the model of a wall and set seven tokens as the attacker’s engines. As long as you can out-think the tokens, the real wall holds.

Second life

Picnickers keep the ants off the cloth with a belt on the grass. A child’s chalk fort holds against the tide for exactly as long as the child stays clever.

First adopted byThe Mozi College gate wardens (a.m. 10); then picnickers.
AncestryGrew from the old story of the Mohist master who unbuckled his belt to show a king why a siege would fail. Grew into Nine Refusals.
Where the burden goesYour posture. You cannot leave the table until the siege ends, and you are holding up your trousers with one hand.
Grand · 8Lan Fang · Mozi College

Nine Refusals 九拒

Every engine raised against a defended city comes apart in the attacker’s hands, nine times in nine ways: a siege tower into numbered planks, a ram into oak and loose iron.

Second life

Demolition crews cast it slowly to save every beam of a condemned hall. The Mozi College casts it in public on anything it judges built for aggression, and there are lawsuits.

First adopted byThe Mozi College, at the gates of three cities in the Silesian year (a.m. 14).
AncestryGrew from Flat-Pack and The Belt Wall. Grew into Unbuild, licensed demolition.
Where the burden goesThe planks remember their places and can be rebuilt; the caster carries the Exertion; and a city remembers who unbuilt its engines.
Wounds · Men on a tower fall with it. The Mohists cast it anyway, because the tower was coming for a city.
Bold · 4Lan Fang · College of Medicine

Fever Bloom 热毒

An attacker is struck by sudden fever. The room spins, the legs go, the blade drops.

Second life

None a physician admits to. Students know the gentlest version as the cure for a bad date.

First adopted byClinic guards in the Workshop Years (a.m. 20s), after a run of medicine thefts.
AncestryGrew from Sweat It Out, run backwards.
Where the burden goesThe caster feels the fever’s echo for an hour. Physicians swear to cast it only in defence; it is registered as a wounding working.
Wounds · 2 Health and a fallen body. Crushing in humid heat, only a dizzy spell in cold air.
Mighty · 16 → 8, sharedLan Fang · College of Medicine

The Recomposed Body 移形术

Surgery without a cut. Shrapnel, a stone or a growth moves out through the tissue as if it were water.

Second life

Sideshow magicians pull coins from volunteers’ elbows with a crude version, and the College prosecutes every one.

First adopted byField surgeons of the paired schools (a.m. 24).
AncestryGrew from the meridian maps and Flat-Pack’s logic of parts.
Where the burden goesThe patient sleeps for two days; the lead physician’s hands tremble for a week.

Toa Keng 大港

The civic university. Its magic lives in maps, ledgers, headlines and rulings, and in the European Rooms, where foreign scholars came to trade and stayed to argue.

Minute · 0Toa Keng · Surveyors’ Hall

Other Angle 换个角度

Step half a pace sideways and see the room as it looks from the doorway, including what is behind the pillar.

Second life

Banned in Toa Keng primary schools during hide-and-seek. Card sharps, press photographers and suspicious spouses swear by it.

First adopted bySurveyors (a.m. 6); then press photographers; then everyone at the card table.
AncestryGrew from the surveyor’s triangulation. Grew into Wrong Map.
Where the burden goesA crick in the neck, and one second of not knowing where you are.
Small · 1Toa Keng · The Press

Fair Copy 誊本

Lay a sheet over a page and the page copies itself onto it, in the original hand.

Second life

Homework, obviously. A love letter copied for three suitors at once was the scandal of a.m. 22, and then a wedding.

First adopted byClerks of the Concord (a.m. 5); then the Press, which printed its first magical edition in a.m. 9.
AncestryGrew from the copyist’s tracing. Grew into the Gazette’s overnight edition.
Where the burden goesThe original fades a shade per copy. The hundredth copy kills the letter, which is why originals are kept in the dark.
Real · 2Toa Keng · The European Rooms

Double Entry 复式

Every debit finds its credit. A stolen purse appears in the thief’s accounts as a weight he can feel in his pocket, growing heavier every hour until he gives it back.

Second life

Lost-and-found counters. Couples who split the bill to the cent. The Counting House’s ‘heavy pocket’ on whoever skips the round.

First adopted byMarket wardens (a.m. 11); then the Counting House; then everyone who has lent a friend money.
AncestryGrew from double-entry book-keeping, brought to the Straits through the European Rooms. Grew into Burden Notes, through the Al-Merikani Trust Houses.
Where the burden goesThe ledger keeper carries a faint weight for every open account. Bankers walk slowly in Toa Keng.
Real · 2Toa Keng · The Press

Headline 头条

Shout one sentence from a rooftop and everyone in the district who cares about it hears it as if read aloud to them. Nobody else does.

Second life

Lost children found within the hour. Hawkers. One public proposal heard only by the person it was meant for, and by her mother.

First adopted byThe Press (a.m. 10); then the Fire Watch, for evacuations.
AncestryGrew from the town crier and the newsboy. Grew into the Gazette’s storm bulletin.
Where the burden goesThe caster loses their voice for the rest of the day. Editors communicate by note.
Bold · 4Toa Keng · Surveyors’ Hall

Wrong Map 错图

An attacker sees the street from the wrong frame: left becomes right, and the alley’s end appears at its start. They run into walls, and off piers.

Second life

Tour guides on drunk visitors. The Toa Keng fair maze, which nobody sober has ever solved.

First adopted byHarbour police (a.m. 16); then the fair.
AncestryGrew from Other Angle, turned outward.
Where the burden goesThe street is uneasy for a day. Shopkeepers give wrong change; cats avoid it.
Wounds · Bruises, sprains, once a drowning. Registered as a wounding working.
Mighty · 16Toa Keng · School of Law

The Precedent 判例

A ruling made in open court becomes true in the substance of things. When the court finds that a river’s water belongs equally to both banks, the river begins, quietly, to share itself.

Second life

None permitted. The School of Law keeps a list of judges who tried, and of the districts they scarred.

First adopted byThe River Court (a.m. 30), first in a quarrel over the northern reservoirs.
AncestryGrew from Double Entry and the surveyor’s boundary stone.
Where the burden goesThe judge. Toa Keng judges age a year for every great ruling, and a bad ruling scars the district it names.

Thread V · 6 spells

Al-Merikani 美里加尼

Mathematicians, astronomers and bankers of the trust. Their magic keeps a thing safe and gives it back on the appointed day.

Minute · 0Al-Merikani · The Trust House

Kept Warm 留温

Entrust a soup’s warmth to its bowl. At the hour you named it is steaming, however long it waited.

Second life

Lunch tins that open hot at noon. Love letters that release their writer’s scent at the moment of reading, and not a minute before.

First adopted bySchoolchildren’s lunch tins (a.m. 7); then the letter-writers of the old quarter.
AncestryGrew from the caravan cook’s banked coals. Grew into The Patient Fountain and Burden Notes.
Where the burden goesThe bowl is cold as stone until the hour comes. Pick it up early and the cold bites your fingers.
Small · 1Al-Merikani · The Observatory

Star Fix 定星

Sight one star through your astrolabe and know where you stand to a hand’s width, even under cloud.

Second life

Children find their way home from the night market by the moon. Couples meet on rooftops without saying which roof.

First adopted byNavigators of the Tarim pairing (a.m. 6); then rooftop couples.
AncestryGrew from the astrolabe’s plate. Grew into Star Road, a whole fleet fixed at once.
Where the burden goesAlmost nothing; the star does not mind. That is why Al-Merikani instruments fetch such prices.
Real · 2Al-Merikani · House of Numbers

Appointed Break 定时之裂

Entrust a crack to a date. The cracked cup holds perfectly until the day you choose, then breaks.

Second life

A rival’s chair entrusted to collapse at the wedding toast. Merchants who guarantee their goods ‘to the day’, and mean it.

First adopted byShipwrights waiting for a refit (a.m. 9); then pranksters; then insurers.
AncestryGrew from Kept Warm. Grew into The Long Deferral.
Where the burden goesThe debt grows while it waits: the longer the deferral, the louder the break.
Bold · 4Al-Merikani · The Garden Court

The Patient Fountain 耐心泉

A fountain holds its water in trust through the dry season and gives one measured cup each hour.

Second life

Courtyards tell time by their fountains. In a.m. 33 a school replaced its bell with a fountain that splashes at the end of each lesson, and others followed.

First adopted byGarden keepers (a.m. 12); then schools.
AncestryGrew from the qanat and the courtyard pool. Grew into Rain on Account with Nine Rain.
Where the burden goesThe cistern below runs cold and smells of old stone.
Real · 2Al-Merikani · House of Numbers

Deferred Blow 迟击

Entrust a punch to the air. It lands three seconds later, after you have stepped away and raised both hands.

Second life

Stage duellists. Schoolyard alibis. A comic routine at the Straits Exhibition of a.m. 34 that ran for a year.

First adopted byCaravan guards (a.m. 13); then the stage.
AncestryGrew from Appointed Break.
Where the burden goesIf you are struck before your blow lands, it lands on you.
Wounds · 1–2 Health, and a reputation.
Grand · 8Al-Merikani · The Trust House

Burden Notes 负担券

A caster’s burden is entrusted to the Trust House, which releases it on a schedule into artefacts, cisterns and the deep sea. Great workings become possible on credit.

Second life

Students borrow exam-week stamina against the summer holidays, and sleep through August.

First adopted byShipyards and great rings (a.m. 21); then students; then speculators, which is a longer story.
AncestryGrew from Kept Warm and Toa Keng’s Double Entry. Grew into The Long Deferral, which no one now casts.
Where the burden goesThe schedule. Every note falls due somewhere, on someone. A Trust House that issues too many is a flood waiting for its date.

Sri Temenggong 天猛公

Keepers of land, coast and body. Their magic asks first, and works with whatever the place says.

Minute · 0Sri Temenggong · The Dusun

Ask First 先问

Ask a tree before you cut a branch, and it lets the branch go cleanly into your hand.

Second life

Durian season: children ask the trees to drop their fruit not on anyone’s head. Parents ask again, on their behalf.

First adopted byOrchard keepers (a.m. 5); then every parent in durian season.
AncestryGrew from the orchard keeper’s greeting. Grew into Mangrove Hold and The Island Speaks.
Where the burden goesYou owe the tree water, a song, a little of your time. Forget, and next season the fruit falls closer.
Grand · 8Sri Temenggong · The Coast School

Mangrove Hold 红树守岸

A coast holds against a storm surge as the mangroves knit their roots into one living wall.

Second life

Kampong children build sandcastles that survive the tide, and the sandcastle contest now has a separate category.

First adopted byCoast villages of the Riau pairing (a.m. 10); then the harbour board.
AncestryGrew from Ask First, asked of a whole shore.
Where the burden goesThe mangroves grow slowly for a season. Asked too often, a shore stops answering.
Small · 1Sri Temenggong · The Loom House

Songket Memory 金丝记

Cloth remembers the body of its wearer and fits it perfectly, however the body changes.

Second life

Hand-me-down baju that refit themselves to the youngest. Detectives who name a stranger from a sarong left behind.

First adopted byTailors (a.m. 8); then large families.
AncestryGrew from the weaver’s measure. Grew into Second Skin, the House of the Body’s bandage cloth.
Where the burden goesThe cloth will not fit anyone else for a year.
Real · 2Sri Temenggong · House of the Body

The Body Remembers 身记

A masseur reminds a sprained ankle how it was before the fall, and it remembers.

Second life

Footballers at half-time. The old men at the coffee shop, whose knees remember a.m. 33.

XII
Thread XII

The rules underneath

Short enough to remember at the table.

A spell is a recipe

Learn it from a teacher, meet its rank and its expendables, and it works. No dice to cast. Your skill decides how well it lands, how cleanly, how beautifully.

Second life is how invention starts

Using a known spell for something its recipe was never written for is an improvisation. Roll your Reach against the World. The World is 1 die for a use inside the same trade, 2 for another faculty of your house, 3 for another house’s trade, 4 for something nobody has tried. Win, and it works this once. Win by three or more, or win three times in a season, and you have found a variant: write it in your house register, under its parent’s name.

Every spell has parents

Each recipe names what it grew from. A variant costs what its parent cost. A spell with parents in two houses is a duet, and must pass both houses’ standards of proof before it is written down. Ancestry is how a house’s songbook grows, and how rivalries start.

The axiom decides what a house can invent

Anyone can learn any perfected recipe, given a teacher. Only Sund can compose a new recipe in which separate systems become one; only Lan Fang a new recomposition; and so on. The discount comes with mastery, and in Sund’s case with numbers.

Sharing, combining and efficiency

Sharing divides the same total among a ring, the leader taking any remainder. Combining pools Reach dice, and a ring of six or more can cast one size above its leader. Efficiency lowers the total itself, and only an axiom grants it.

Affinity comes from three places

House +1, faculty +1, your own specialism +1, to a limit of +3. A Forge Hall metallurgist and a Keel Yard architect are both Sund, and fight very differently.

Harm is real

A wounding spell lists its harm on its card and takes that Health from its target. Wounding workings are registered under the Concordat. Casting one outside defence or licence is a crime, and the first step on the road to villainy.

Every spell names its sink

The burden lands somewhere you can see: the air that frosts, the harbour that steams, the linens that are boiled, the ring’s aching legs, the sea lane the fish abandon. Sinks change with place. A sink asked too much becomes a Scar.

Each house decides what counts as proven

A crew and a storm for Sund; a hundred patients or chairs for Lan Fang; fifty published trials for Toa Keng; seven named witnesses for Al-Merikani; three monsoons for Sri Temenggong; one full cycle for Nine Rain; three generations for Sané-Kora. They argue about each other’s standards constantly.
