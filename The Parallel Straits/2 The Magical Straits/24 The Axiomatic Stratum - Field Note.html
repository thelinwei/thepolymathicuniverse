§0 · METHOD BEFORE MAGIC

A small ledger of honesty

The equations in this document are not claims that the video contains formal mathematics. They are a contemporary bridge: a way of making the fictional theory explicit enough to examine.

The first conversation began before the video itself had been inspected. Once the actual file was available, one important correction became clear: 相律层 is not best read as a thermodynamic “phase layer.” The video’s own English subtitle calls it the Axiomatic Stratum.

Video

What the source establishes

Manifest and axiomatic strata; local and temporary rewriting; axiomatic perception/contact/resistance tolerance; different magical tendencies; world resistance; invocation; and the warning that a quiet measurement need not mean safety.

Bridge

What modern formalism can clarify

State functions, restoring potentials, response profiles, measurement error, control theory, latent variables, constraint satisfaction, and relations represented as edges rather than merely properties of objects.

Conjecture

Where our own magic begins

The exact equations, the distinction between resistance and returning load, the “API / request” analogy, and the broader relational ontology are interpretive extensions developed from the video’s conceptual machinery.

§2 · TWO STRATA

Reality is what appears.
Magic touches what makes appearing possible.

The crucial shift: magic is not described primarily as moving things in the visible world. It intervenes in the relations underneath the visible event.

The 显相层 — Manifest Stratum — is the world of observable events. A ball falls. A body occupies a position. A door marks a boundary. Beneath it, the 相律层 — Axiomatic Stratum — contains the relationships and conditions from which those manifestations arise.

Plate I · manifestation as a consequence of axiomatic structure
M = F(A)
How to read it

A denotes the underlying axiomatic / relational structure. M denotes manifest reality. The equation does not claim that the video gives a literal function F; it makes explicit the video’s causal direction: what appears depends on a deeper arrangement of relations.

Magic becomes less like “adding an invisible force” and more like editing a condition of possibility.our formal bridge
§3 · WHAT A SPELL DOES

Local. Temporary. A rewrite.

The video’s phrasing is unusually disciplined: magic is framed as a local, temporary rewriting of the Axiomatic Stratum — not an unlimited suspension of reality.
Plate II · the magical intervention
A′ = A + ΔA ⇒ M′ = F(A′)
How to read it

A spell perturbs the underlying relation. The visible effect is downstream. Stopping a falling ball is therefore not conceptually identical to applying a hidden upward push; it may be a brief change in how this ball participates in this relation to force.

That makes the fictional ontology much more interesting than the common fantasy equation wizard + mana + spell → effect. The operative unit is not simply an object. It is a relation. The magician reaches beneath the event and modifies the rule by which the event becomes possible.

§4 · TALENT WITHOUT A MANA TANK

Talent is a profile, not a quantity

The video explicitly mocks the idea that one person simply has “more magic” stored inside the body — no bottle of fire behind the ribs, no folded road stored in the stomach.

Magical aptitude is decomposed into distinct capacities. This already makes the world feel more like a science of individual response than a fantasy power ranking.

01

感律Axiomatic Perception

The ability to sense the Axiomatic Stratum — to detect a force changing before motion occurs, or perceive spatial relation beyond what ordinary perception discloses.

02

触律Axiomatic Contact

The ability to connect, however briefly, with the Axiomatic Stratum and influence one of its relationships.

03

承抗Resistance Tolerance

The ability to endure the reaction produced when the world is altered — not simply “having more power,” but surviving the consequence of intervention.

Plate III · aptitude as a vector
T = (P, C, R, a)
Our notation

P = perception, C = contact, R = resistance tolerance, while a is a vector of affinities or tendencies. The point is conceptual: talent occupies several dimensions at once.

elemental response偏向元素回应
speed & motion速度和运动
distance & boundaries距离和边界
emotion, memory & attention情绪、记忆与注意

Two people can therefore be equally “gifted” and still be incomparable. One may perceive exquisitely but intervene weakly. Another may make powerful contact while barely understanding what they touch. A third may survive enormous resistance. The fictional science produces specialisation naturally.

§5 · WORLD RESISTANCE

The world does not punish.
It returns.

The lecture frames world resistance not as hostility or moral judgement, but as the tendency of an existing axiomatic structure to restore stability.

This distinction is beautiful because it removes intention from the universe. Reality is not offended that it has been altered. It simply has structure — and structure has restoring tendencies.

Plate IV · one possible restoring model
V(A) = (k/2)||A − A₀||² ; R_world = −∇ₐV
Bridge, not source

Think of A₀ as an established relational configuration and V as a stability landscape. The farther a spell pushes the system from that configuration, the stronger the restoring tendency may become.

Plate V · resistance is not necessarily identical to what the caster feels
Lᵣ = G(Rw, caster, affinity, mode of contact)
Conjectural distinction

World resistance can be treated as a property of the disturbed configuration; returning / resistance load can be treated as the portion that appears in the caster’s experience or instrumentation. This separation helps explain why “different affinities settle the account differently.”

§6 · THE ZERO THAT IS NOT NOTHING

安静不等于安全

One of the most science-like moments in the whole lecture is not a grand law. It is an anomaly: an intervention that produces almost no measurable returning load.

Calm does not necessarily mean safety.

The warning matters because it introduces epistemic humility into the fictional science. The instrument is not the phenomenon. An absent reading need not mean an absent process.

L_measured ≈ 0 ⇏ L_actual ≈ 0

That single move gives the world a history of science: observation, instrument, model, anomaly, revision. The theory is allowed to encounter something it cannot yet explain.

The magic feels scientific not because its laws are ours, but because its epistemic behaviour resembles science.phenomenon → model → anomaly → better model
§7 · INVOCATION

Not every spell commands

The lecture distinguishes invocation / prayer-like magic because it does not merely order a relation to change. Through words, intent, and resonance, it asks the world to respond.

This creates a second mechanism inside the same universe: one can intervene directly in a relation, or one can establish conditions under which the world itself accepts or sustains a relation.

Direct manipulation

CASTER → ΔA “change this relation”

The practitioner reaches the axiomatic relation and alters it.

Invocation / 祷告

CASTER → request / resonance WORLD → response → ΔA

The practitioner asks, aligns, resonates, or satisfies conditions under which the world responds.

Plate VI · from imperative control to constraint satisfaction
direct: u ↦ ΔA ; invocation: conditions satisfy permission ⇒ ΔA
Analogy only

This is where a computational analogy becomes delicious: direct magic resembles issuing an instruction; invocation resembles making a request that succeeds only if a deeper set of conditions is satisfied. The “law may grant permission.”

§8 · WHY THIS LANDED SO HARD

The unit of magic may be the edge, not the node

This is where the video leaves ordinary fantasy territory and brushes against relational ways of thinking about reality.

A ball is not magically interesting merely because it is a ball. What matters is ball ↔ force. A door is not simply a door; it participates in person ↔ distance ↔ boundary. Memory can be treated through its relation to attention.

In graph language, conventional object-centred thinking starts with nodes and adds relationships afterward. This magic system increasingly suggests the inverse possibility: the manifested character of a node depends on the configuration of edges in which it participates.

Plate VII · the ontological inversion
things → relationships ? ⟶ relationships → things-as-manifested
The deeper question

The video does not explicitly state this philosophical inversion. But its examples make the question available: are relations secondary descriptions of already-existing things, or do things become what they are partly through the relations that constitute their manifestation?

Still open

What exactly is a “relation” here?

Logical rule? Physical coupling? Informational constraint? Ontological primitive? Different answers would produce radically different magical sciences.

✦ WORKING NOTE · NOT A FINISHED CLAIM ✦
Magic is not interesting because it breaks the laws of reality. It becomes interesting when we ask what kind of reality would make magic lawful.

That is the turn the video makes possible: from spectacle to ontology, from “power” to relation, from a fantasy effect to a science capable of being wrong about its own instruments. We stop asking what spell was cast. We begin asking what relationship was touched.
