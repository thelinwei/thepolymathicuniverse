THE PARALLEL STRAITS · MAGICAL ATLAS · VOLUME II

七种魔法文明Seven Grammars of the Axiomatic Stratum

One magical physics. Seven civilisational sciences. Fire still burns, stones still float, minds can still be heard — but the seven Schools disagree, productively and sometimes violently, about what relation was actually touched.

The aim is not to invent seven exclusive “elements.” It is to preserve the familiar repertoire of fantasy and superhero powers while giving each culture a distinctive causal grammar beneath the same manifestation.

§0 · FREEZE THE LAYER

What we have actually discovered

Volume I reconstructed the video’s Axiomatic Stratum. Volume II asks how entire civilisations would learn different portions of the same relational physics.

The breakthrough is deceptively simple: a power class and a magical mechanism are two different things. Fireball, healing, mind-reading, teleportation, telekinesis and weather control can remain recognisable across the world. The diversity appears beneath them.

Shared physics

What the world permits

The Manifest Stratum, the Axiomatic Stratum, local/temporary rewriting, world resistance, aptitude and affinity remain common facts of nature.

Common repertoire

What people see

Fire, lightning, telekinesis, healing, telepathy, illusion, invisibility, clairvoyance, teleportation, summoning, spirit-work, probability, time, wards.

Civilisational grammar

What people think they are doing

Each School has learned to notice certain relations, lower resistance along certain pathways, and encode those insights into rituals, devices, institutions and magical technologies.

Magic is shared by all. Culture determines which relationships people have learned to notice.the core design principle

§4 · THE SEVEN FOLIOS

Not seven elements.
Seven magical sciences.

None of these abilities is biologically racial. They are institutional lineages: learned methods, archives, rituals, instruments, habits of perception and accumulated research.

A student can cross traditions. A Malay practitioner may become Lam Keng’s greatest recompositionist; a Chinese practitioner may inherit Temenggong custodianship; a Kora-trained scholar may specialise in spatial magic. What the School transmits is not blood. It is a way of seeing relations.

01 · TKI / PARALLAX

多视 · 转译 · 视差Toa Keng Institution

“From which description is this impossible?”

Strong domains

Information · Space · Mind · Pattern

Favoured operations

Perceive · Transform · Exchange

Outsider shorthand

ESP · clairvoyance · illusion · telepathy · portals

Historical seed → fictional evolution

Port-city adaptation, cultural translation and contact language become a magical epistemology of overlap. TKI scholars learn that two descriptions can both be locally valid — and that the difference between them is itself actionable information.

The School’s old archives treat translation as a laboratory: every mistranslation exposes hidden structure.

Signature I

Surveyor’s Sight

Triangulates an unknown relation from several known ones. Begins as distance-sensing; matures into remote viewing, route inference and portal geometry.

distance-sense → clairvoyance → remote observation → topological navigation
Signature II

Creole Casting

Translates another School’s spell into an equivalent relational description. TKI becomes disproportionately good at hybrid teams, cross-school research and counter-magic.

translation → compatibility → hybrid spellcraft
Signature III

Double Exposure

Allows two perceptual or positional relations to coexist. Advanced practitioners can make two viewpoints overlap without collapsing them into one.

illusion → shared viewpoint → layered cognition
Failure mode: Frame Dissolution. Too many simultaneously valid descriptions can erode the practitioner’s ability to commit to a single actionable world.
02 · AL-MERICAN / AMANAH

عهد · 见证 · 承诺Al-Merican College

“A relation witnessed is harder to break than a relation merely desired.”

Strong domains

Law · Identity · Space · Spirit

Favoured operations

Bind · Exchange · Restore

Outsider shorthand

wards · summoning · sanctuary · seals · oath-magic

Historical seed → fictional evolution

Indian Ocean mercantile culture, jurisprudence, protective inscription, calligraphic and mathematical-esoteric practices become a magical science of permission, obligation and witnessed relation.

It is important in-world that this is not “Islam = magic.” The School’s magical tradition emerges historically beside religious, legal, mathematical and commercial institutions — and develops its own independent science.

Signature I

Amanah Seals

Wards specify conditions as well as forming walls: the invited may cross; hostile intent is held; iron may pass while flame is stopped.

ward → conditional ward → permissions architecture
Signature II

Caravan Roads

Repeated exchange creates strong place-to-place relations. Old trade routes become easier to traverse magically than arbitrary space.

route memory → fast travel → trusted-route teleportation
Signature III

Resistance Underwriting

Backlash can be distributed across consenting parties, pledged reserves, talismans or future obligations. Magical finance is born.

load sharing → risk pooling → magical insurance
Failure mode: covenant debt. The stronger and more witnessed the relation, the more catastrophic its breach may become.
03 · SRI TEMENGGONG / COMMUNION

地忆 · 守护 · 归属Sri Temenggong Institution

“This land remembers what you have forgotten.”

Strong domains

Life · Identity · Place/Space · Force

Favoured operations

Perceive · Restore · Bind

Outsider shorthand

healing · plants · animals · weather · spirits · geomancy

Historical seed → fictional evolution

Maritime sacred-place traditions, keramat, custodianship, old routes, graves, trees, stones and sites become a science of accumulated relation. A place is not empty coordinates: it is a dense historical field.

Authority therefore comes from maintenance and relationship, with ownership a lesser claim.

Signature I

Pusaka Resonance

Heirlooms accumulate relational density through repeated ownership, use, ritual and memory. The object becomes easy to contact because it has a thick history.

object memory → identity anchoring → ancestral implements
Signature II

River Memory

A buried river, vanished shoreline or former forest can be called toward an earlier relational configuration. Historical ecology becomes literal magical force.

geomancy → ecological recall → landscape restoration
Signature III

Healing by Belonging

Restores relations among body, self, kin and place along with the tissue. Certain conditions respond differently depending on where and among whom the patient is held.

healing → relational diagnosis → belonging medicine
Failure mode: false custodianship. Claiming relation where none has been maintained produces unreliable magic — sometimes violent resistance from the site itself.
04 · LAM KENG / RECOMPOSITION

化 · 构 · 迭代Lam Keng College · 南景书院

“There may be no original spell — only transformations that have not yet admitted their ancestry.”

Strong domains

Matter · Force · Life · Law

Favoured operations

Transform · Create · Move

Outsider shorthand

fire · lightning · telekinesis · transmutation · constructs

Historical seed → fictional evolution

Chinese cosmological change, talismanic and ritual traditions, practical medicine, diagram, craft and later industrial experimentation combine into the world’s most unapologetically engineered magical science.

Five Phases are treated as a grammar of transition: one configuration generating, constraining or transforming another.

Signature I

Wuxing Conversion

Finds lower-resistance pathways between states: chemical potential to heat, heat to pressure, pressure to motion. A fireball becomes an optimised cascade.

elemental magic → transformation chain → energy architecture
Signature II

Fu Arrays

Encodes relational operators into repeatable diagrams. What one wizard once had to hold consciously becomes executable magical infrastructure.

spell → notation → device → industry
Signature III

Meridian Engineering

Reinterprets inherited body maps through anatomy, instrumentation and cybernetic feedback. Old concepts are tested as candidate relational models.

healing tradition → measurement → bio-magical engineering
Failure mode: recomposition without lineage. Their genius for adaptation repeatedly creates crises of authorship, extraction and ownership.
05 · HOUSE OF NINE RAINS / RECIPROCITY

循环 · 时机 · 回馈House of Nine Rains

“Nothing happens alone, and every miracle enters an account.”

Strong domains

Life · Force · Pattern · Space

Favoured operations

Exchange · Restore · Perceive

Outsider shorthand

weather · growth · ecology · divination · seasonal magic

Historical seed → fictional evolution

Maya calendrical and agricultural ritual traditions, together with Andean knot-recording and highland reciprocity, are kept as distinct ancestral sciences that later encounter one another inside the House.

Their synthesis is not “pan-Indigenous magic.” It is a historically specific institutional achievement built from several lineages that continue to retain their differences.

Signature I

Calendar Magic

Acts when reality is already nearing the desired transition. Nine Rains practitioners lower resistance by choosing the moment when the system is ready to move.

forecast → timing → phase-sensitive intervention
Signature II

Khipu Computation

Knotted records become a medium for tracking magical exchanges: water borrowed, heat displaced, labour given, resistance deferred, ecological debt carried forward.

record → magical accounting → auditable ritual
Signature III

Ceque Fields

Works at the scale of territorial graphs. Sites become nodes; ecological, sacred and logistical relations become edges.

site magic → network magic → watershed / territorial systems
Failure mode: feedback. A local success can create a remote ecological or temporal debt that returns years later.
06 · KORA / THE THIRD VOICE

记忆 · 名称 · 缺席Kora House

“A missing voice still changes the harmony.”

Strong domains

Mind · Information · Identity · Pattern

Favoured operations

Perceive · Restore · Exchange

Outsider shorthand

telepathy · memory · psychometry · ancestry · sound

Historical seed → fictional evolution

Mande jaliya — oral history, genealogy, praise, mediation and the kora — becomes a magical science of identity carried through witnessed account. The House also inherits a deep respect for orally transmitted customary law and assembly memory.

Its great insight: deletion does not always erase structure. An absence can remain relationally visible.

Signature I

Praise-Name Magic

A name is treated as a compression of ancestry, action, witness and relation. Correctly naming someone can stabilise identity against possession, memory alteration or shape-change.

genealogy → identity anchoring → anti-possession / anti-erasure
Signature II

The Counter-Song

Detects relational inconsistencies left by magical erasure: the empty pronoun, the missing beat, the room arranged for five when memory insists there were four.

psychometry → absence detection → historical reconstruction
Signature III

Polyphonic Telepathy

Does not collapse minds into one voice. It holds several accounts simultaneously, preserving contradiction as part of the signal.

telepathy → plural cognition → mediation / interrogation
Failure mode: ventriloquism. Hearing traces does not grant the right to speak for them. Projection is one of Kora’s oldest professional dangers.
07 · SUND / COMMONS

共持 · 法定人数 · 维护The Sund Commons

“Ask not who cast the enchantment. Ask who has been carrying it.”

Strong domains

Law · Force · Life · Matter

Favoured operations

Exchange · Bind · Restore

Outsider shorthand

shields · group healing · repair · shared powers · infrastructure

Historical seed → fictional evolution

Assembly traditions, communal law, cooperative infrastructure and later egalitarian political cultures become a magical science in which the group relation itself is an operator.

The old “thing” assembly matters here as one historical precedent for law, assembly and public decision becoming ritually spatialised.

Signature I

Quorum Casting

Some works cannot be performed alone because the relationship among participants is the thing being manipulated. Ten isolated casters are not equivalent to ten coupled casters.

group spell → relational operator → superlinear coordination
Signature II

Load Balancing

World resistance is distributed according to capacity. The Commons can sustain works that would kill a solitary practitioner.

shared load → adaptive distribution → civic-scale magic
Signature III

Maintenance Magic

An enchantment persists because generations inspect, repair, clean, remember and care for it. Maintenance becomes a magical act.

care → persistence → infrastructural enchantment
Failure mode: coerced commons. Forced participation, hidden labour and unequal burden can produce apparently stable magic whose social resistance is accumulating invisibly.
§5 · WHAT THIS DOES TO THE WORLD

The spell keeps its ontology hidden

A battlefield observer may correctly identify “telekinesis” while completely misunderstanding the mechanism, source of risk, and likely countermeasure.

This creates strategic, scientific and philosophical consequences. Counter-magic requires diagnosing the grammar along with the effect. Cross-school teams become powerful because one School can perceive the blind spot of another. Magical espionage becomes an epistemological problem.

Plate IV · one manifestation, many causal models
same manifestation ⇏ same intervention
Consequence

“What happened?” and “how did it happen?” become separate investigative questions. A magical forensic scientist must infer grammar from residue, resistance pattern, relational scars and failure behaviour.
