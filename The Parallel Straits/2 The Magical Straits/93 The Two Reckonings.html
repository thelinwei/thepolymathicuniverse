[The Two Reckonings]两场清算
(a war fought to unmake rather than kill, a crash built on burden sent to people who never agreed, and the charter that came after both)

On the anniversary, every school in the Straits teaches one lesson without speaking.

Claude1 November 2026 · companion to The Lamp Years

The outcomeTwo wrongs, one law

The war and the crash are the same wrong in two forms: harm sent where nobody consented to carry it. The Straits answer both, and host the rules for everyone.

  1. The Empty Desks. After the Silesian Breaking, Konrad’s scattered court brings its grievance to Veyr, the toll port at the Narrows of the Sound. Its arsenal faction seizes seven Straits teachers and twelve students and tortures them for destructive magic. None will give it. It steals their books and learns to run gentle recipes backwards.
  2. The Senyap Works. The houses turn their children’s martial classes into corps, and gather their composers on a quiet island to make magic that breaks things, under one doctrine: unmake, do not kill.
  3. The Battle of the Sound. One night ends the war: a fleet unbuilt in silence, its rulers asleep, its stolen knowledge taken back, its land resting. Fewer than three hundred dead.
  4. The Falling Due. Antwerp, Genoa and Osaka sell Clean Paper that sends burden to places that never agreed, until it all falls due on one quarter day.
  5. The Monsoon Charter. The houses steady the world and host its new rules, one half for war and one for trade, headquartered in the Straits.

Thread I

Veyr at the Narrows 狭海之维尔

A toll port on the Sound, and the road from the Silesian Breaking to the atrocity.

Veyr sits at the Narrows of the Sound, the strait that is the gate into the Baltic. For centuries it lived on the dues every passing ship paid, and it was a respected exchange port, paired with Sund’s Norden schools. The road from there to the Empty Desks runs through Silesia.

1931西里西亚之断
The Silesian Breaking

Prince Konrad, lord of the Oder coal measures, tries to hold a whole province by magic and breaks himself doing it. His court scatters. Its officers carry one conviction away with them: the Straits schools let their prince break, and kept the real magic for themselves.

the years after军械派
The arsenal faction

Konrad’s engineers and officers find work and welcome in Veyr, whose toll trade is failing now that wind-roads let ships sail past without stopping. They bring a creed: advanced learning is a Straits cartel, and a port that cannot break things will be broken.

three times三请
The demands

Veyr asks the Concord for licences to compose harbour-breaking workings, for defence. Three times the Concord refuses, and says why: the schools teach only what makes and mends.

one season狭海之扰
The Narrows incidents

Veyr’s guard boards Concord ships in the Sound and seizes their artefacts for inspection. The Long Song carries the protests; Veyr returns the artefacts, broken open.

one night议会之夜
The council taken

The arsenal faction wins Veyr’s council in a single night of votes and threats. The old toll families sail for Copenhagen. The new head of the council takes the old title, Warden of the Narrows.

that winter邀请
The invitation

The Warden invites an exchange delegation to teach civic defence and artefact renewal. The schools send one, because they have always believed that teaching is how tensions end.

Thread II

The Empty Desks

Seven teachers, twelve students, eleven weeks.

Seven Straits teachers and twelve of their students went to Veyr on exchange, through Toa Keng’s European Rooms and Sund’s Norden pairings. They taught shipyard rings, fever medicine, star-fixing, memory strings, rain cords, the asking of land, and law.

The Warden had other plans for them. One winter night his guard closed the exchange house, and the teachers were taken to the old toll house on the quay. For eleven weeks they were beaten, starved and kept from sleep, and asked one thing: how does your magic break a ship, a wall, a man? Each of them had only ever taught how to make and mend. Each refused in their own house’s way.

The seven teachers and five of the students died there. Seven students were sent home in the spring, as a message. The law student’s record came home separately, on cigarette papers in a laundress’s basket. The courts record it as the Veyr Exchange Murders. The schools call it the Empty Desks, 空桌: every chair the dead left stays empty for a full academic year. The Straits remember it as the Veyr Silence, 维尔之默, and on its anniversary every school in the port teaches one lesson without speaking.

Sundforge master

He taught them how a ring shares one weight, and nothing else, all the way to the end. The ring he left them can lift a beam. It cannot drop one.

Lan Fangphysician

Before the cell door opened she recomposed her own notebooks back into clean paper, page by page. They found a ream of blank sheets and a woman who smiled at them.

Al-Merikaniastronomer

His notes were entrusted to a date and a place. They open only in the Straits, on the day of his return. The jailers hold a locked book they will never read.

Sané-Korajeli and teacher

She had sung her lessons into memory strings. When they took the strings, she sang one last note and every string forgot what it held.

Sri Temenggonghealer

The ground of the compound would not let the jailers sleep. Every night of her captivity, the floors of their own barracks refused them.

Nine Raincord-keeper

Her cords read only with the song that goes with each knot. Without her voice they are just knotted string, and she did not sing.

Toa Kenglaw student

The youngest of them. With Fair Copy he wrote the whole thing down, every name and every day, on cigarette papers, and passed it out through a laundress. It is the only record.

III
Thread III

What they took, and what they made of it

Constructive magic, read backwards.

Backwards magic 逆术

What they took was constructive magic, so they ran it in reverse. Sweat It Out became Fever Bloom on a whole street; Everyone’s Shoulder, reversed, became a weight dropped on a crowd. Every Straits recipe names its parents, so every Straits recipe can be read backwards to its harm.

Cruder, costlier, and theirs 粗而贵

Without instruments, expendables or a house’s standard of proof, the reversed recipes work badly and cost three times over. The Warden does not care. Veyr sinks the burden into the old Oder estates, and later into other people’s ports.

What they never got 所失

The Al-Merikani book stays locked. The Sané-Kora strings stay silent. The Lan Fang notebooks are blank. The best of Straits magic never leaves the cell, and the Warden knows it.

From the children’s class to the corps

Every house always had its sports and martial arts. Now they put on armour.

For a generation, magic in the schools’ martial classes was like taekwondo for nine-year-olds: belts, forms, a bow at the start and the end. After Veyr the classes become corps, and the forms become drills.

SundRing wrestling, after Norse glíma

For children. Children learn to lift and throw as a pair, then as a ring of four.

For war. Ship-ring boarding drill: forty sailors move as one body across a deck.

Lan FangKung fu and the Mozi gate drill

For children. Children learn forms and the old Mohist siege games on a sand table.

For war. The Mozi defence corps, who can unbuild a siege tower while it is still rolling.

Toa KengFencing, from the European Rooms

For children. Children learn the bout, the bow, the rules.

For war. Frame-fencing: a duellist steps half a pace sideways in the world and is somewhere else.

Al-MerikaniTahtib stick-dance and archery

For children. Children learn the stick-dance as a festival game, and to shoot by the stars.

For war. Archers who loose a flight of arrows entrusted to land a minute later, from every direction.

Sri TemenggongSilat

For children. Children learn the flowing forms, and to ask the ground before they stand on it.

For war. Silat on ground that agrees with you and refuses your opponent.

Nine RainThe ball game and the Andean runners’ trials

For children. Children play the hip-ball game and race the mountain relays.

For war. Relay runners who carry a working from hand to hand across a valley faster than a horse.

Sané-KoraLaamb wrestling and dambe boxing

For children. Children wrestle in the sand to drums.

For war. Fighters who wrestle in a pair and strike with the third thing between them.

VI
Thread VI

The Senyap Works

The schools’ Manhattan Project.

On Pulau Senyap, a small island in the eastern Straits, the seven houses build the Senyap Works, 静岛工坊. Composers come from every house and every paired school, sworn to silence. The Works is chaired by the dean of the Mozi College, because the Mohists have always built weapons on one condition: only for defence, and only to make a war end sooner.

Their charge is new for every one of them. Make magic that breaks things. Then their doctrine, argued over for a year: unmake, do not kill. Break the works, silence the rulers, take back the knowledge, deny the magic, make the land refuse, and kill only when there is no other way.

The first test is the Hush, cast on an empty islet. It overreaches. For an hour the whole port goes dark: cold lamps die, ferries drift, a kite-ferry comes down in the harbour. Every person in the Straits feels, for one hour, what the world was before the Kindling. The islet has been silent ever since, and the island the Works stood on took its name from it: Senyap, the quiet one.

Toa Keng × Sund

IX
Thread IX

The Battle of the Sound

The last night of the Closed Harbour War: the fleet meets the Straits at the gate of the Baltic, in the strait the Sund house takes its name from.

The raids掠港

Veyr’s Narrows Fleet sails with its backwards magic and its stolen artisans. It takes Danzig, then Riga and Visby, carrying off workshops, artefacts and craftsmen the way Timur carried off the weavers of Damascus. Then it turns for Copenhagen and Sund’s Norden partners, and the Straits come to meet it at home, in the Sound.

The Hush寂

The Straits and their paired schools meet the fleet in the Sound. The Hush falls first. Every artefact on every ship dies at once, and for the first time in a generation those sailors are in a world without magic.

The unbuilding拆

The Mozi corps unbuilds the fleet ship by ship, from the bow. Sund ring-boarders pull the sailors from the water in chains of forty. They pull out most of them.

The far strikes远击

On the same night, by Long Song and Pattern Ring: Rust Tide in the Veyr yards; the Long Sleep on the Warden and his war council; the Forgetting on every composer of backwards magic; the Drought Account on Veyr’s reservoirs; Ground Says No on the old Oder estates that feed its army; and, a week later, the Appointed Breaks.

The morning晨

Veyr wakes without a fleet, without its rulers, without its stolen knowledge and, for a season, without magic. Fewer than three hundred dead on both sides, in a war that could have cost tens of thousands.

The new order

After the Sound, the Straits are the most trusted power in the world. Ports queue to join the paired schools, and the rules that come next are written in the Straits’ own hall.

The Three Exchanges

A consortium of ports too eager to grow rich at others’ expense.

Our world knows the pattern: profit kept at home, cost sent somewhere nobody is looking. In finance it is debt passed on; in the air it is carbon. In the Straits world it is burden.

Antwerp

The oldest exchange in the north, built for trading in promises. It issues the paper.

The House of St George

Genoa

The bankers who lent to kings. It underwrites the paper and lends against it.

The Dojima Exchange

Osaka

Where rice was first traded as a promise of future rice. It invents the bundles.

Thread XI

Clean Paper

How to cast all day and feel nothing.

Clean Paper 净券

A certificate that a working’s burden has been sent somewhere else. A merchant in Antwerp casts all day, buys Clean Paper, and feels nothing.

The sinks 汇

The burden goes to places nobody asked: a mountain valley, a reef, the tundra, a fishing island, the poorer quarters of distant cities. They are told nothing, so they agree to nothing.

Bundles 包

Osaka’s invention. A thousand certificates are bundled together and graded Deep, Fair and Shallow by how much more each sink is supposed to hold. Nobody buying a bundle knows which valleys are in it.

Deferral 延

An imitation of the Al-Merikani Trust Houses: burden entrusted to a future date, then rolled forward again, and again. Most of the bundles fall due on the same quarter day.

The Falling Due

From boom to quarter day.

The boom繁

Clean Paper makes the consortium’s cities the most comfortable in the world. Cold lamps on every street, season banks in every cellar, no one tired. Other ports buy in to keep up.

In our world · Tulip mania in Holland, 1636–37, and the South Sea and Mississippi bubbles of 1720.

The first signs兆

A reef off the Coromandel coast turns white. A valley in the Carpathians sleeps through harvest. Shallow bundles are quietly sold on to people who cannot read them.

In our world · The years before 2008, when mortgage bundles rated safe were not.

The quarter day到期

The deferred burden falls due everywhere at once. Sinks already full overflow into their neighbours. Frost in July over the lowlands; the tundra thaws and smokes; on three islands every person sleeps for a week; artefacts across half a continent die as the renewal banks that held Clean Paper fail.

In our world · The crash of 1929; and the thawing permafrost of our own century.

The panic慌

Clean Paper is worthless overnight. Cities built on it feel, for the first time, the full weight of their own magic. Ports that never bought any are hit hardest, because they were the sinks.

In our world · Toxic waste shipped to Koko, Nigeria, in 1988; offsets that offset nothing.

XIII
Thread XIII

Holding the world steady

Each house marshals its network.

Al-Merikani
The Trust House confesses

Burden Notes were Al-Merikani’s invention before they were anyone’s weapon. The Trust House says so in public, opens its vaults, and becomes the lender of last resort, taking bad paper in exchange for time.

Sund
The Great Ring

Sund calls for volunteers across every paired school. A hundred thousand people stand in rings for a month and share the overflow, a gram each. It ends on the Ninth Night with the World Holds One Night: millions of households light one lamp and give one almost imperceptible measure each, keeping medicine, water, food and shelter running until dawn. The custom of the Ninth Night lamp begins there.

Nine Rain
Rescheduling the sky

The Cord House turns the falling-due into a cycle: debts paid back over seven seasons instead of one night, each season’s share small enough for a sink to bear.

Toa Keng
The Great Ledger

Toa Keng traces every certificate to its sink and publishes the list: who cast, who profited, which valley paid. For the first time the world can see where its magic went.

Sri Temenggong
Asking the harmed

Sri Temenggong goes to the reefs, valleys and islands and asks what they need, and the answers become the terms of repair.

Lan Fang
Recomposing the ground

Lan Fang recomposes thawed tundra and frost-killed fields back into living soil, slowly, by the hectare.

Sané-Kora
The choirs

Sané-Kora sends choirs to the islands that slept. The third voice wakes them gently, a village at a time.

XIV
Thread XIV

The Monsoon Charter

Rules of engagement for the world, headquartered in the Straits.

Two disasters, one law. The Monsoon Charter, 季风宪章, is signed in the Convocation Hall where the Kindling happened. It has two halves, because the war and the crash turned out to be the same wrong: harm sent where nobody consented to carry it. Its rules are known as the Straits Articles. The Straits host the Charter; the world governs it.

In war

Teachers are protected

Teachers and students of magic, at home or on exchange, may not be held, harmed or questioned in war. Named for the Empty Desks.

No working on a mind without consent

Except sleep, for a named person, for no longer than a season, to stop a working already harming others.

Written by the Works’ own composers, who knew these workings best.
No Hush on a people

Magic may be denied to a fleet, an army or a works, for no longer than a season, and never to a whole city.

The buoyed edges of the Hush in the Sound become the standard.
Land may refuse, never be poisoned

No working may stop land growing for longer than three seasons.

Stolen knowledge is returned

Recipes taken by force are returned, and their makers named.

Every wounding working is registered

And every one used in war is reported to the Court within a moon.

In trade

Every working names its sink

No working may be cast without a named place for its burden.

No sink without consent

A place, a people or a species must agree to carry burden, and may withdraw.

The ledger is public

All burden held in trust is recorded in one open ledger in the Straits.

Deferral has a limit

No burden may be deferred beyond one generation, or rolled forward more than once.

Proposed by the Trust House itself.
No bundles nobody can read

Every certificate names its sink, and travels with a witness from that sink.

Lines in italic mark rules drawn from the Senyap Works’ own practice.

Notes for alignment

Where this meets the other pages.

Working notes

Veyr at the Narrows is the aggressor, first proposed in GPT’s 99. The Silesian Breaking is the precedent that escalates into it. An earlier draft of this volume placed the aggressor at Stettin.

The first war of magic. PMU-bm had no World Wars. This one is regional, short and Baltic-centred, and it ends the long peace in a single night.

This replaces the earlier Falling Due. The first draft of the Lamp Years blamed a syndicate inside four Straits houses. Here the crash is the consortium’s, built on an Al-Merikani invention that the Trust House then puts right.

The Charter binds everyone, the Straits included, and several of its rules come from the Senyap Works’ own practice.

Dates are loose. The war sits in the Workshop Years, and the crash at their close, around 1947, where 91 places the Falling Due. The Charter follows. Names can change.
