I · The Conjecture

1. The Seven May Not Be Native

The neat version would be seven Schools descending from seven lost home worlds. The richer version is messier: the Seven are domesticated remnants of foreign physics, accumulated, translated, mixed and made mutually survivable by the Straits over centuries.

The Seven therefore need not correspond one-to-one with seven primordial worlds. Many worlds, lineages and causal systems may have crossed into the Straits. Most vanished. Some merged. Some became outlaw traditions. Some became ordinary culture. Seven large compatibility families eventually stabilised.

$$ A_1,A_2,A_3,\ldots,A_n \xrightarrow{\text{translation in the Straits}} G_1,G_2,\ldots,G_7 $$

Seven may therefore be real and constructed at the same time.

II · A New Definition of Magic

2. Magic May Be Foreign Physics Under Local Conditions

In its native world, a law is not magic. It is simply reality.

In a world where names animate matter, naming is not spellcasting. In a world where perspective alters embodiment, perspectival transformation is not supernatural. In a world where time can be faced as direction, temporal turning is mechanics.

Bring that causal grammar into the Straits and it must be actively sustained.

$$ \boxed{ \text{magic} = \text{non-default law locally emulated} } $$

World Resistance can then be understood as the Straits asserting its own default law:

“That is not how things work here.”

The caster replies:

“For the next seven seconds, it is.”

The cost of magic becomes the cost of causal mismatch.

$$ L \propto d\!\left(A_{\text{local}},A_{\text{invoked}}\right) $$

Where \(L\) is load and \(d\) is the mismatch between the local Axiomatic Stratum and the law being held in place.

Spellcasting becomes temporary ontological jurisdiction. A caster creates a bounded region in which another causal order is permitted to operate.
III · The Host World

3. Why Can the Straits Host Foreign Law?

Most adjacent worlds seem heavily specified. Their Axiomatic Strata decide which kinds of relation are physically real.

The Straits appears different.

Its native law may permit relationships to be stabilised, leaving their form open.

$$ A_{\text{Straits}}: \qquad R(x,y)\ \text{may be locally held} $$

This makes the Straits less a world of one strong axiom than a world with unusually low axiomatic closure.

Other worlds

Reality says what a relation is allowed to be.

The Straits

Reality permits a relation to be held, translated and negotiated—at a price.

This could explain why so many Seams accumulate there, why foreign grammars survive there, why load exists, and why Concordance could emerge there.

The Straits is not special because it contains diversity.

It contains diversity because its physics is capable of hosting foreign law.
IV · Migration

4. Physics Becomes Culture

Imagine the earliest arrivals.

A population crosses from another world. Their bodies and habits were adapted to laws that now need deliberate support. Their ordinary practices fail. Their children fall sick. Tools behave incorrectly. Social relations lose the force they once had.

They discover that certain combinations of material, speech, geometry, memory, sequence, architecture and social conduct can partially recreate the conditions of home.

Those procedures become ritual.

Later generations forget the reason.

Foreign physics · an ordinary law in another world
Survival practice · deliberate recreation of that law in the Straits
Ritual · repeated procedure whose original technical logic is forgotten
Culture · identity forms around the practice
School · the practice becomes institutionalised, taught and mythologised
$$ \boxed{ \text{foreign physics} \rightarrow \text{survival practice} \rightarrow \text{ritual} \rightarrow \text{culture} \rightarrow \text{School} } $$

The Seven may therefore be memory systems for causal orders that need care to operate in the Straits.

Geometry, acoustics, planting, materiality, circulation, inscriptions and ceremonial sequence could all reduce the mismatch between the Straits and a School's ancestral law.

$$ L_{\text{inside School}} < L_{\text{outside School}} $$

$$ A_{\text{Straits}} \rightarrow A_{\text{Straits}} + \varepsilon A_{\text{foreign}} $$

Near such an object, the local world bends slightly toward the foreign Axiomatic Stratum.

The relic may not be sacred because the founder touched it. The founder may have become important because the relic made their reality survivable here.
VI · Folklore as Technical Ruin

6. Myth May Be Degraded Documentation

Founding stories can now be read as broken technical records.

“The founder crossed the sea carrying fire in a bowl.”

Later interpretation: sacred flame.

Possible original meaning: a portable pocket of foreign thermodynamics carried through a Seam.

“Our ancestors followed a star that did not move.”

Later interpretation: celestial omen.

Possible original meaning: a foreign astronomical body visible through a persistent Seam.

“The first keeper asked the land for permission.”

Later interpretation: humility before place.

Possible original meaning: migrants required a stable host relation before their bodies could adapt.

Nonsense syllables in a school song

Later interpretation: archaic liturgy.

Possible original meaning: phonetic calibration sequence in a lost language.

Folklore becomes the archaeology of physics.
VII · Why Seven?

7. There Were Probably More Than Seven

If the Straits has accumulated foreign laws for centuries, the official number seven becomes suspicious.

Many causal grammars may have crossed.

Some failed to reproduce. Some generated intolerable load. Some became household practices. Some merged. Some were erased. Some survive as outlaw or shadow traditions.

The Seven may therefore represent stable compatibility classes.

$$ n\text{ imported axioms} \rightarrow 7\text{ stable translation families} $$

This allows several apparently contradictory historical theories to be simultaneously valid:

Historical theory

Many migrations occurred; institutions later consolidated them.

Mystical theory

Seven genuine modes recur beneath the historical variation.

Political theory

Seven is an administrative settlement imposed upon a larger ecology.

Mathematical theory

Seven may be a minimum basis sufficient to span the Straits' local relational possibility-space.

Seven is real.

Seven is constructed.
VIII · Orthodoxy

8. Shadow Traditions May Preserve the Originals Better

Official Schools may not be the purest descendants of ancestral physics. They may be the safest translations.

A shadow lineage could therefore be more faithful to the foreign law while being far less compatible with the Straits.

$$ \text{authenticity} \not\Rightarrow \text{survivability} $$

This destabilises the language of corruption and orthodoxy.

The outlaw may be more original.

The institution may be more livable.
IX · Grey and Clear Reinterpreted

9. Two Failures of the Compatibility Layer

The Grey

Axiomatic decoherence.

No single causal regime fully governs an object, identity or relation. Translation loosens without replacement.

$$ A_{\text{local}} \rightarrow \text{indeterminate mixture} $$

The Clear

Axiomatic monopoly.

One causal regime achieves complete dominance. Everything interoperates because everything finally obeys the same law.

$$ A_1,A_2,\ldots,A_n \rightarrow A^\* $$

The Settled becomes especially chilling under this model.

It is not dead magic.

It may be a world in which causal mismatch has vanished.

No World Resistance. No load. No magic.

Because reality and expectation have finally become identical.
X · Scale

10. Powerful Magic Means Larger Axiomatic Territory

High-level magic may not mean “more energy.” It may mean a larger volume of reality temporarily governed by a non-default law.

$$ \text{spell scale} \sim V_{\text{foreign law}} $$

A great relic can therefore be terrifying without behaving like a weapon.

It may cause an entire valley to obey another world's causal rules.

Granary field

Every grievance acquires causal force.

Lettered field

Unnamed mechanisms cease functioning.

Regard field

Embodiment becomes perspectivally unstable.

The greatest magical weapon attacks the rules the object thought it lived under.
XI · Exchange

11. Returning to an Ancestral World

A student from one of the Seven travels to a world whose physics resembles their School's ancestral grammar.

All their life, magic required concentration, cost and World Resistance.

Then they arrive.

They perform the familiar action.

Nothing glows.

Nothing strains.

The world simply complies.

For the first time in their life, reality feels effortless.

Some students would experience this as homecoming.

Others as betrayal.

The living culture of the ancestral world may also find the diaspora School bizarre:

“Why are you doing that ritual?”

The School believed the ritual sacred. In the origin world, it may be unnecessary because the law it recreates in the Straits is simply ambient reality there.

This creates diaspora identity at cosmic scale.

XII · Reverse Exchange

12. The Foreign Student Discovers Magic

A student from an adjacent world may have never considered themselves magical.

They cross into the Straits.

Suddenly every ordinary competency becomes costly.

“Why does speaking correctly hurt here?”

What a School calls elite magical technique may be a foreign visitor's everyday embodied literacy.

This would puncture enormous amounts of institutional prestige.

The tradition carries the physics.

XV · The Sevenfold Harbour Rewritten

15. The Harbour May Have Been Becoming Seven Worlds at Once

Normally a School operates as a low-intensity translation of ancestral law.

$$ G_i \approx \text{local approximation of }A_i $$

During the Harbour catastrophe, every School pushed beyond normal limits.

$$ G_i \rightarrow A_i $$

For several hours, the ancestral physics began to reinstantiate.

The harbour was being held in seven laws at once.

It was becoming governed by seven causal regimes simultaneously.

$$ A_1\parallel A_2\parallel A_3\parallel\cdots\parallel A_7 $$

That is why every lineage's observations were correct.

And that makes the First Concordance almost impossibly sophisticated.

$$ \boxed{ A_1,A_2,\ldots,A_7 \rightarrow \text{cohabitation without overwrite} } $$
XVI · The Concordat Rewritten

16. A Constitution, Runtime and Cosmological Ceasefire

The Concordat can now be read as several things at once:

Political constitution

No School may become sovereign over reality.

Compatibility layer

Foreign grammars can coexist without overwriting one another.

Safety architecture

Each School remains separate enough to preserve difference and connected enough to share one city.

$$ \boxed{ \text{difference sufficient for persistence} + \text{translation sufficient for coexistence} } $$

Too much separation produces fracture.

Too much interoperability produces convergence.

The Concordat may therefore be the most sophisticated magical technology ever created.

A civilisation.
XVII · The Seven Human Interfaces

17. The Protagonists May Possess Weak Axiomatic Locking

Most people become strongly calibrated to one School's compatibility grammar.

Our seven do not.

Their institutions read this as poor fit, impurity, indecision or unreliability.

But it may actually be:

$$ \text{low axiomatic locking} $$

They can tolerate movement between causal systems without immediate collapse.

True Concordance may therefore be physiological and cognitive as well as ritual.

$$ \boxed{ C = \text{multi-axiomatic homeostasis} } $$

They become different.

They become capable of remaining themselves while reality changes its rules around them.

A Hinge across laws as well as histories.

XX · Ultimate Conjecture

20. What If the Straits Never Fully Crystallised?

Most worlds may be stable solutions drawn from \(\Omega\).

$$ \Omega \rightarrow A_i $$

But perhaps the Straits is unusual because the crystallisation never fully closed.

$$ \Omega \rightarrow A_{\text{Straits}} \quad\text{with residual openness} $$

Its defining property may be a capacity to host axioms.

This does not make the Straits superior.

It may make it unstable, expensive and exhausting.

Reality itself requires negotiation there.

The Straits is a passage.

A narrow relational corridor where realities that should not be mutually habitable can remain together long enough to change one another.

The deepest version of the Seven

The Seven were never teaching seven ways to do magic.

They were teaching seven incompatible realities how to remember themselves without destroying their neighbours.

The deepest version of Concordance

$$ \boxed{ \text{Concordance} = \text{difference becoming intimate without becoming identical} } $$

At the scale of seven students.

Seven Schools.

Seven ancestral realities.

And perhaps, eventually, an entire pluriverse.
