Seven Schools
Worn objects & adornment.

GPT26 October 2026

Seven ways to carry, gather, listen, remember and belong.

Seven collections, forty-three objects: bags, clasps, combs, cuffs and keepsakes that carry each school’s character from the uniform into the hand.

The proposition

A school’s character, held close to the body.

Belonging enters daily life through small gestures: opening a bag, fastening a wrap, gathering hair, holding a message or sitting down to listen. These collections extend the schools’ uniforms and emblems through those gestures.

Each collection has its own voice, found in materials, construction and use.

School 01 · Toa Keng Institution

Parallax Instruments

Displacement becomes a way to observe. Offset planes, narrow sightlines and unfolding surfaces translate the school’s split lozenge into objects for inquiry.

Institutional belief

Knowledge begins in the ground we actually stand on.

Point of departure

Clothing that transforms into furniture and back again sets the starting point: accessories that change configuration and invite a second reading.

Object 01

Parallax Satchel

Displaced charcoal panels open around a teal interior; a sliding bridge crosses the gap.

Object 02

Sightline Cuff

Two angular metal elements frame a narrow opening beside the ear.

Object 03

Field Folio

Hinged panels unfold to hold maps, paper and field notes.

Object 04

Offset Fastener

A metal bridge joins split forms across a recessed teal line.

Object 05

Datum Ring

Stepped metal planes carry interruption and alignment around the finger.

Object 06

Archive Loop

A textile wrist strap holds a removable location, observation or personal record.

Development record
First study. The satchel and fastener carry the identity strongly. A later folio could develop a more substantial transformation into a supported drawing surface.

School 02 · Al-Merikani College

Objects of Keeping

The travelling scholar carries things entrusted to their care. A semicircular case and a radial organiser bring the geometry of navigation into the act of keeping.

Institutional belief

Knowledge travels through relationships that must be worthy of trust.

Point of departure

Cross-cultural craft and geometric wood inlay anchor the collection, joined by astrolabe-inspired copper construction and the ingenuity of Renaissance instrument-makers’ sketches.

Object 01

Route Case

A deep semicircular indigo case carries copper arcs, engraved scales and a pivoted pointer.

Object 02

Orbit Clasp

Concentric metal rings gather ivory cloth around an indigo inset.

Object 03

Keeping Box

A wood-inlaid vessel opens to a lined interior for a letter or personal token.

Object 04

Bearing Earpiece

An open brass arc follows the ear, with pale insets and an indigo accent.

Object 05

Promise Bracelet

Two geometric clasp halves complete a pattern across a woven indigo strap.

Object 06

Traveller’s Fold

Radial pocket leaves open around a hub to organise notes, cards and a drawing tool.

Development record
Revised plate. The Route Case is more semicircular, with copper astrolabe detailing; the Traveller’s Fold now opens radially. The Bearing Earpiece and the other three pieces carry over.

School 03 · Sri Temenggong Institution

The Living Lattice

Rigid supports cradle soft enclosures. Bamboo and wood gather cloth, hold belongings and frame the body through open, breathable-looking structures.

Institutional belief

Continuity is an obligation to care for what sustains life.

Point of departure

Inherited bamboo craftsmanship, carried into contemporary sculpture and functional work, ties the collection to Sri’s living inheritance and custodianship.

Object 01

Canopy Bag

Two woven bamboo shells cradle a removable forest-coloured cloth pouch.

Object 02

Leaf Clasp

Curved laminated bamboo planes gather olive and cream folds.

Object 03

Root Comb

Dark wooden teeth meet a smooth, rising bamboo arch.

Object 04

Seed Vessel

A small woven capsule holds a personal keepsake on a soft cord.

Object 05

River Cuff

An open bamboo lattice spans the wrist between smooth end bands.

Object 06

Fold Sling

Curved wooden supports give a soft olive carrier its folded compartments.

Development record
First study. The Canopy Bag establishes a strong silhouette. The leaf clasp’s proposed joining mechanism remains a useful next construction study.

School 04 · Lan Fang College

The Unfinished Archive

An archive continues through its revisions. Folding pages, displaced seams and interrupted frames bring departure and return into the worn object.

Institutional belief

Belonging can survive departure, translation and reform.

Point of departure

Changing, body-following drapery opens an exploration of soft collapse, asymmetry and accessories that stay open to transformation.

Object 01

Page Bag

A scholar’s satchel opens into pale expanding compartments beneath an offset flap.

Object 02

Broken Seal

An interrupted silver ear cuff is punctuated by a cinnabar connection.

Object 03

Margin Pocket

A shoulder-suspended document pocket hangs beside loose scholarly clothing.

Object 04

Revision Ring

Displaced edges meet across a small red connection.

Object 05

Return Pin

A forked hairpin suggests the swallow through an opening in its silhouette.

Object 06

Private Seal

A small stone pendant holds a personal carved mark developed over a student’s years.

Object 07

Fold Wrap

Pleated ink and parchment textile wraps the wrist beneath a metal stitch-like bar.

Development record
First study. The Page Bag, Margin Pocket and Broken Seal were identified as promising. A later pass could reduce the printed distressing and let folds, joins and contrasting materials carry more of the identity.

School 05 · The House of Nine Rain

The Carried Field

Terraces organise what is carried; channels connect separate parts. Earth and fibre bring landscape, records and provision into everyday adornment.

Institutional belief

Sky, water, land and livelihood are one connected problem.

Point of departure

Material-led work with plant fibres, farming communities and biodiversity grounds the collection in the relationships between land and livelihood.

Object 01

Terrace Sling

Three staggered woven compartments meet a terracotta fastening with a turquoise channel.

Object 02

Rain Link

Articulated clay segments carry a continuous watercourse around the ear.

Object 03

Field Record

Knots, beads and woven strands form a personal record bracelet.

Object 04

Water Clasp

Two terraced ceramic halves complete a glazed channel when brought together.

Object 05

Seed Wallet

Earth-toned woven fields fold around card pockets and a small clay toggle.

Object 06

Horizon Comb

Stepped ceramic and turquoise inlay sit above broad dark wooden teeth.

Development record
First study. Rain Link and Water Clasp establish continuity across separate parts. A future bag pass could offset the compartments more boldly.

School 06 · Sané-Kora Institute

Between Voices

Listening is given a place on the body and on the ground. Open loops, hand-held vessels and shared sitting rituals extend the school’s dialogic authority.

Institutional belief

Knowledge is carried between voices and tested through response.

Point of departure

Sound, sensory experience and collective memory form the brief, carried into the school’s own textile and metal workshops.

Object 01

Response Mantle

Open copper loops suspend and gather the shoulder wrap.

Object 02

Listening Arc

Reimagined as two compact copper ribbons wrapping the upper ear in a helix cuff.

Object 03

Palm Memory

A hinged wooden vessel holds a small message in a textile-lined interior.

Object 04

Voice Sash

A woven diagonal band carries a discreet wooden capsule for notes.

Object 05

Common Ground

A shoulder-carried textile roll opens into a personal sitting and listening mat.

Object 06

Answering Pair

Two distinct collar pins: a call and its response.

Development record
Revised ear-cuff plate. A compact double copper cuff around the upper ear replaces the earlier lower arc. Common Ground and the Answering Pair suggest student rituals.

School 07 · The Sund Commons

Common Parts

The joint becomes the ornament. A shared vocabulary of rails, frames and inserts connects a bag, a clasp, jewellery and the tools needed to care for them.

Institutional belief

A shared structure endures through the contribution of every member.

Point of departure

Thinking about the ecological, historical and political conditions of design frames the collection’s materials, production and continuing responsibility.

Object 01

Common Carrier

Removable canvas panels hang from an ash rail through exposed metal connections.

Object 02

Reciprocal Clip

Metal frames and a small wooden insert form an ear clip.

Object 03

Joint Clasp

A protected concrete inset bridges two brushed steel members.

Object 04

Repair Band

A replaceable wooden link joins a woven wrist strap.

Object 05

Pocket Workshop

A folding cloth kit carries tools, spare fasteners, thread and patches.

Object 06

Shared Links

Interchangeable steel, wood and protected concrete elements become a key tether.

Development record
First study. Pocket Workshop makes care part of belonging. Visible screws and replaceable parts carry the school’s ethic of repair.

A tradition of making

Each school invites a maker to develop a ceremonial object, then works with students and craftspeople to translate its ideas into everyday wear. Over decades the commissions form a lineage that every generation revises.
