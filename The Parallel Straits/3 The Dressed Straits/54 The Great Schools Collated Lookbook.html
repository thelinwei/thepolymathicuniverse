Shared brief · governs the next pass

The uniform includes the space it gives you.

Coverage, volume, bodily definition, gender access, suspension and motion are independent design choices.

Body and cloth

A waist may be natural, raised, dropped, selective or absent. Cloth may follow, suspend, envelop, gather or release. Shared access across genders does not require compulsory sameness. Each school retains its own values and construction language.

People

Show teenage students with varied healthy builds and everyday individuality. Include slender and slightly waifish students without equating sport with a sculpted physique. Consider hair, head coverings, footwear and posture as part of the whole person.

Translation

Resolve the chosen silhouette before multiplying it into occasions. Use front, side, rear, seated and moving views. Translate construction rather than merely printing a motif onto the same shirt and shorts.

Movement

Start from the activity and the wearer’s coverage choices. Secure folds and tails; clear hems; test reach, sitting and stride. A concept rendering does not establish a sewing pattern, tropical comfort or sport performance.

Next review

Review this consolidated edition before further look generation. Choose the families to retain or refine, then revise student casting and construction within each school’s authorised scope. Junior/senior grading remains separate from assembly/everyday/sport.
