This record gathers the environmental-design work of the morning of 27 October 2026: six emerging architectural grammars and the corrections that made each one more particular. Four later Nine Rain studies join them.

Al-Merikani, Sri Temenggong, Lan Fang and Sund have found their own systems: astronomy as civic mechanism, a timber village growing through trees, a joint organising an entire campus, a school colonising inherited machinery. Nine Rain and Sané-Kora carry compelling atmospheres, and their next iterations travel further from the works that first prompted them.

School 01 · Al-Merikani College · Astronomical campus

The clock tower lies down and becomes the school plaza.

A future archaeology of masonry, copper instruments and time made civic.

Strong baseline · Compact monumentality
The proposition

Al-Merikani occupies a wooded hillside away from the main streets. Aged masonry frames coexist with copper mechanisms and visibly newer insertions. The entrance remains mysterious and compressed; the internal plaza opens into a horizontal observatory of dials, shadows and sunken astrolabe machinery.

Rules carried forward
  • Heavy, deeply historical masonry without oversized civic grandeur.
  • A high narrow door creates the dramatic threshold.
  • Copper is inset as mechanism, aperture lining and astronomical emblem.
  • The central dial radiates starburst routes into surrounding buildings.
  • Libraries and laboratories share a soaring mechanical spine, with intimate rooms at its edges.
Design lineage

Future-archaeological weight and material memory meet moving copper instruments and the horizontal clock. The language lives in the tension between the two.

Prompts · Lina Ghotmeh · Santiago Calatrava · Leonardo’s machines

School 02 · Sri Temenggong Institution · Arboreal campus

Its rooms negotiate with every trunk and canopy.

A medieval timber fort grows into a lifted kampong village, then acquires a lighter engineered-wood future.

Strong baseline · Old and new timber lineages
The proposition

Older dark-shingled timber buildings form the fort-like core. Classrooms perch high, conjoin across bridges and wrap around existing trees. Later additions use pale engineered timber, paper-like shells and more elaborate learning and sports spaces.

Rules carried forward
  • The campus remains primarily wooden and assembled as a village.
  • Older, darker structures and newer pale additions stay distinguishable.
  • Classrooms may perch, bridge and grow around mature trees.
  • Interior and exterior learning overlap constantly.
  • Modern spaces can become elaborate without losing the intimate kampong scale.
Design lineage

A lifted kampong and a medieval wooden fort meet structural lightness and replaceable systems. The later spaces extend the village in engineered timber around its darker inherited core.

Prompts · Shigeru Ban

School 03 · Lan Fang College · 兰芳书院 · Tectonic campus

The joint is permanent; the members it holds may change.

斗拱 is the architecture, from campus scale to furniture scale.

Active ideation · Generative system emerging
The proposition

Interlocking bracket logic generates flying roof fields, columns, bridges, shelves, seats, drainage and classrooms. A later paper-tube study makes the structure lighter and more replaceable and keeps the tradition alive.

Rules carried forward
  • Use 斗拱 as a recursive load-bearing system.
  • A joint should be traceable from roof to column to furniture.
  • Flying roofs behave as a flock or a field.
  • Different generations of timber, metal and tube remain visible.
  • Replaceable members allow the school to become an archive of construction knowledge.

The next exterior study can grow stranger still: roofs that spiral, bifurcate, turn vertical, bridge between buildings or seem to migrate. The Paper Canopy and the Folded Walk already show the more autonomous architecture.

Design lineage

斗拱 brings recursive joint intelligence; paper-tube members bring lightness, modular assembly, replaceability and long spans. Together they make a living tectonic tradition.

School 04 · House of Nine Rain · Hydrological campus

A building that receives the monsoon before it receives the student.

The school is reached across water, and rainfall is the architecture’s operating condition.

Baseline established · Further departure required
The proposition

Nine Rain sits beside a naturalised river on its own wooded ground. Students cross a roofed bridge to reach a complete circular aperture. Oversized troughs, cascades, bioswales and planted terraces make heavy rainfall legible and safe.

Rules carried forward
  • The entrance circle must remain complete and unobstructed.
  • The bridge is part of the arrival ritual.
  • Water channels must be deliberate enough for extreme rain.
  • The school and river form one landscape.
  • Next: move farther from the initial monumental references.

A / The morning studies

The river, the roofed approach and the hydraulic façade hold. The four later studies carry the brick, the circle and the water further: a façade that funnels, a court that cascades, an atrium that filters light, a hall that looks through its own wall.

B / Brick, water and light · four later studies

Design lineage

Intimate, climate-conscious interiors meet geometric mass and water-bearing public façades.

Prompts · Marina Tabassum · Louis Kahn

School 05 · Sané-Kora Institute · Breathing campus

The roof breathes; the public room listens.

Earthy and porous at its edges; unexpectedly sculptural, chromatic and resonant within.

Baseline established · Further transformation required
The proposition

Low earthen blocks and inhabited verandas make most of Kora intimate. Detached double roofs, protected apertures and ridge vents make air movement visible. Only the House of Voices becomes monumental.

Rules carried forward
  • Most spaces stay low, shaded, communal and tactile.
  • Ventilation must be legible in section and experience.
  • Sculptural concrete appears as one adapted layer within the campus.
  • Coloured light arrives as restrained reflections.
  • Next: grow the reference DNA into a language of Kora’s own.

The modest Long Shade and the soaring House of Voices give Kora its spatial range. The next study keeps the climate intelligence and the acoustic idea and lets the campus grow a language of its own.

Design lineage

Earth, transitional shade and roof ventilation meet unusual roof forms, reflected colour and one monumental room.

Prompts · Francis Kéré · Le Corbusier

School 06 · The Sund Commons · Industrial campus

Every new programme derives its form or behaviour from the industrial structure it occupies.

The school colonised the shipyard’s machine.

Strong baseline · Site-specific system found
The proposition

A decommissioned Sembawang shipyard fabrication complex supplies the monumental scale. The school itself arrives as smaller parasite rooms, catwalks, ramps, gardens and transforming mechanisms.

Rules carried forward
  • The inherited machine provides scale; the school remains intimate.
  • Old steel and new insertions stay visibly distinct.
  • Tanks generate concentric rooms; cranes generate movement; docks generate tidal learning.
  • Pipes carry rainwater, planting and services rather than becoming decoration.
  • Every intervention should be reversible, repairable and educational.
Design lineage

Tactile, transforming mechanisms meet cuts, ramps, suspended rooms and circulation as spectacle. The shipyard itself is the primary author.

Prompts · Heatherwick Studio · Diller Scofidio + Renfro

Morning conclusion

Six baselines.
Two departures still to find.

Al-Merikani belongs to its instruments, Sri Temenggong to its timber village, Sund to its shipyard, and Lan Fang is beginning to belong to its joint. Nine Rain belongs to the river and Sané-Kora to air, shade and voice; both are passing through one more transformation toward an architecture wholly their own.

A reference earns its place when it releases a new system.
