01 · Where the reference belongs

Give this to Kora; keep Temenggong’s folded regalia.

The supplied plum ensemble offers Kora a more layered, cloth-led presence than its earlier sculpted cape: a broad shoulder crossing, rich woven edges, an anchored sash, wrapped headwear and soft footwear. These parts make a coherent new family.

Temenggong can keep the contrasting emerald-and-gold court silhouette, short shoulder cape, angular kain fold and firm waist clasp. Both use folds, but their silhouettes and material accents remain distinct.

Kora · a voice carried by cloth

Rounded crossings, a long vertical core, soft layers and a visible woven border. The emblem is a stitched relationship between two loops, with no need for a hard pendant or clicking ornaments.

Temenggong · an anchored court fold

A compact upper body, sharper geometric waist construction, defined gold border and a folded headpiece. Its metallic clasp has a different visual and tactile job.

No whole-school swap is necessary. This direction makes the wrapped ensemble Kora’s active ceremonial direction. The earlier plum cape remains an archived alternative; it is not automatically reassigned to another school. The other six school directions stay in play.

02 · Ceremonial development

Keep the romance of the wrapping.

The ceremonial study keeps the long layered silhouette. More ordered sash ends, deliberate border placement and a legible waist anchor make the draping rich and intentional.

Material & colour

Mulberry and aubergine with warmer plum layers, fine ivory-toned woven edges and a quiet ivory under-tunic. Cloth depth and finish carry the richness.

Headwear & footwear

A modest wrapped band leaves the curls visible. Soft dark closed slippers continue the quiet material language. These are design choices, open to every wearer.

03 · Translate the family

Reduce the layers; retain the crossing.

Broad shoulder wrap→Fixed assembly panel→Everyday yoke→PE inset / athletic waist fold

The uniform carries a part of the headmaster’s cloth. Its identity lives in the diagonal crossing, one vertical pleat rhythm, a narrow border and the soft double-loop mark.

04 · The emblem

A soft fastening.

The two loops retain their own outlines and meet through a shared opening. This proposed wearable mark can be embroidered into the sash or used as a soft loop fastening on assembly clothing.

This exact vector drawing defines the prototype geometry. Small generated motifs may vary; they should not be treated as production artwork or replacements for an existing school crest.

05 · What this changes

Kora becomes recognisable through a crossing.

The earlier Kora family was led by a rounded cape contour. This branch makes a wrapped shoulder and ordered woven border the primary signature. The quiet material behaviour and soft loop mark remain useful continuities.

That gives the school an identity which can travel from a layered ceremonial ensemble to a clean everyday shirt, without needing every wearer to carry a robe. The athletic branch then moves the gesture again, into a shoulder inset or secure waist fold.

Strongest parts to carry forward

The headband with visible curls; the broad shoulder crossing; the deep plum / ivory relationship; and the possibility of a soft emblem that actually participates in fastening the garment.

Open for the next iteration

Which rendered uniform best preserves the reference; whether the assembly panel should end at waist or upper thigh; how much pleating to keep; and whether junior students use shorts, another lower silhouette, or the same trousers.
