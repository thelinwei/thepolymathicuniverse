A speculative institutional design for Singapore

The ConcordanceCommonwealth

Intellectual lineage, formal model, spatial architecture and research agenda for four historical educational roots, three revisable ports, five territorial cantons, one thin commons and a protected outside.

DocumentWorking Paper 01
StatusExploratory · not policy advice
AuthorsLin Wei × ChatGPT
Date1 September 2026
Abstract

This paper formalises a speculative alternative to Singapore’s unitary school governance: four educational polities rooted in the island’s historical Chinese-, Malay-, Tamil- and English-medium lineages; three time-limited foreign or experimental “Ports”; five territorial cantons; and a thin federal Commons that guarantees rights, equity, portability and the continued possibility of disagreement. The model’s distinctive claim is not merely polycentric provision. It introduces Concordance—joint action without compulsory commensuration—and a protected Interval: time, space and practice that educational institutions may not fully capture. A \(5\times7\) crossing structure prevents educational worldviews from acquiring exclusive territories. The paper distinguishes historical evidence, established theory, project synthesis, formal definitions, design conjecture and fictional stress tests; proposes mathematical representations; identifies failure modes; and outlines a staged research programme. It does not claim that seven ministries are presently desirable or feasible. It asks what institutional variety Singapore would need if long-term adaptability, epistemic plurality and life beyond schooling were treated as constitutional goods rather than optional enrichment.

Scope warning

The terms Root, Port, Commons, Interval, Concordance and Clear are project constructs. Their value lies in how precisely they help generate and test hypotheses—not in sounding inevitable. The four historical language streams are a documented substrate; transforming them into contemporary educational constitutions is a speculative design move, not historical restoration.

1 · research problem

How much difference can a small state constitutionally maintain?

And how can it maintain that difference without converting it into race, class, postcode, brand prestige or administrative chaos?

The standard policy question asks which educational intervention works best. This project asks a prior systems question: under uncertain futures and plural values, should the state preserve several institutional capacities to decide what “works” means?

That question produces five linked design problems:

A · VARIETY

Difference with teeth

Multiple systems must have enough autonomy to make consequentially different decisions—not merely different school cultures.

B · EQUITY

No enclave privilege

Mobility, funding and admissions must prevent institutional plurality from hardening into inherited advantage.

C · ACTION

Decision without unity

The systems must still act together under deadlines, emergencies and shared infrastructure.

D · LIMIT

Life beyond capture

The entire society must not become educational territory merely because learning can happen everywhere.

2 · intellectual lineage

A confluence, not an invented genealogy.

The project does not descend neatly from one tradition. It sits where Singapore history, Chinese relational thought, polycentric governance, pluralist politics, cybernetics, ecology, critical pedagogy and design for the pluriverse begin to interfere with one another.

Singapore · historical

Four language streams as institutional memory

Singapore formerly contained English-, Chinese-, Malay- and Tamil-medium schools with differing sponsors, curricula, social bases and political histories. English became the common first-language medium across schools by 1987. The proposal retrieves the historical fact of institutional plurality without proposing ethnic re-segregation. Project move: ancestry belongs to institutions, not pupils.

Tay Kheng Soon · Singapore

Competing ministries and the school in civic life

Tay’s canton thought experiment proposed several governments, HDBs and MOEs whose competition might generate innovation. His “Super School” work argued for integrating learning environments with community space. Project move: separate territorial cantons from non-territorial educational polities, then cross them.

Chinese thought · resonance

和而不同, 中和, 君子不器

和而不同 offers the concise possibility of harmony without sameness. The Zhongyong distinguishes 中 and 和 as relational achievement rather than a bland average. 君子不器 resists reducing cultivated persons to single-use vessels. Project move: these are resonances and provocations, not claims that classical texts contain the proposed constitution.

Dewey · Tay · pedagogy

Learning embedded in social life

Dewey’s school-as-community and Tay’s civic “nervous system” both oppose the isolated school parcel. Project move: “learning in the thickness of life,” while rejecting the totalising slogan that the entire city is a school.

Illich · institutional critique

Learning beyond schooling

Illich’s critique of institutionalised education and proposal for learning webs help reveal the danger that every human capacity becomes a credentialled service. Project move: a constitutional Minimum Unschoolable Life, protected even inside a society rich in learning.

Ostrom · governance

Polycentric authority at multiple scales

Ostrom’s work challenges the assumption that complex commons require either one central authority or privatisation. Polycentric units can exercise substantial independence while nesting coordination. Project move: five territorial authorities, seven educational authorities and a limited federal layer with explicit domains.

Berlin · Mouffe · politics

Incommensurable values and legitimate conflict

Berlin’s value pluralism rejects the assumption that all genuine values fit one hierarchy; Mouffe’s agonistic politics rejects consensus as the erasure of conflict. Project move: Concordance, where a common act and its unreconciled dissents occupy the same legal instrument.

Escobar · Glissant

Pluriverse, relation and opacity

Escobar foregrounds relational world-making and autonomous, place-based design; Glissant defends relation without the demand for complete transparency. Project move: the Interval protects difference from compulsory translation and capture.

Scott · legibility

What administration destroys by seeing

Scott shows how standardisation and administrative legibility can erase practical knowledge and complex local interdependencies. Project move: Rumah Arus’s routes become the canonical stress test—saving the basin requires making knowledge legible, which also makes it expropriable.

Ashby · Holling · systems

Requisite variety and resilience

Ashby relates regulatory capacity to the variety of disturbances; Holling separates persistence under change from narrow equilibrium stability. Project move: institutional redundancy is treated as costly adaptive capacity, not automatically as waste or virtue.

Central synthesis

No cited thinker supplies the whole architecture. The distinctive contribution is the combination: historically rooted and rotating educational polities; cross-cutting territorial membership; dissent-preserving joint action; and a positive quantity of life with no educational owner.

3 · system definition

Two maps, one island, thirty-five crossings.

The core anti-segregation move is to stop educational sovereignty and territorial sovereignty from sharing the same boundaries.

T

Territorial cantons

\(T=\{t_1,\ldots,t_m\}\), provisionally \(m=5\). Responsible for place: planning, housing, public realm, local services and infrastructure.

S

Educational polities

\(S=R\cup P\), where \(R\) contains four historically rooted systems and \(P\) three revisable Ports. Responsible for pedagogy, staffing and institutional practice.

F

Federal Commons

A thin common layer for rights, equity, safety, minimum civic foundations, portability, translation, shared data constraints and emergency coordination.

U

Protected Interval

A nonempty domain of time, space and practice where no educational polity may impose compulsory capture, even though ordinary law and safeguarding continue to apply.

3.1 The crossing graph

Represent the institutional field as a bipartite graph \(G=(T\cup S,E)\). The strong version requires every educational polity to maintain a meaningful presence in every territorial canton:

$$E=T\times S,\qquad |E|=|T||S|=5\times7=35$$
Each edge is a Canton College network, not necessarily one building.

The complete bipartite graph (K_{5,7}) is a design ideal, not a demand for identical replication. Edge weights (w_{ij}) may represent learners, staff, facilities, course-hours or public investment. A weak or nominal edge does not count as a substantive crossing.

3.2 Allocation and cross-cutting exposure

Let \(x_{ij}\) be the number of learners belonging to educational polity \(s_i\) while residing in territorial canton \(t_j\). The matrix must satisfy both territorial and system totals:

$$\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_{ij}=N_j,\qquad \sum_{j=1}^{m}x_{ij}=M_i,\qquad x_{ij}\ge0$$
\(N_j\): learners resident in canton \(j\). \(M_i\): total membership of system \(i\).

Presence alone is insufficient. For each canton, define the system share (p_{ij}=x_{ij}/N_j), Shannon entropy (H_j), and the effective number of represented systems (D_j):

$$H_j=-\sum_{i=1}^{n}p_{ij}\ln p_{ij},\qquad D_j=e^{H_j}$$
If all seven are equally present, \(D_j=7\). This is a diagnostic, not a moral target.

A normalised islandwide crossing index can be written:

$$X=\frac{1}{m}\sum_{j=1}^{m}\frac{H_j}{\ln n},\qquad 0\le X\le1$$

High (X) does not prove equity. A system can be demographically present yet socioeconomically segregated. If (Q) denotes income, class or another protected social dimension, mutual information (I(S;Q)) provides one warning signal: strong predictability of School from class indicates that plurality is becoming stratification. It should be interpreted with direct distributional and qualitative evidence, not used alone.

Design proposition P1 · conjectural

Cross-cutting membership reduces territorial essentialisation only if mobility is substantively affordable.

The graph does not solve sorting by itself. Admissions, transport, housing, fees, language support and prestige must be designed so that \(E=T\times S\) describes lived access rather than marks on a governance chart.

3.3 Roots and Ports operate at different temporal speeds

The Roots are slow variables: historically sedimented institutions with long memory, internal dispute and a presumption of continuity. Ports are fast variables: experimental systems with fixed review horizons, no claim to permanence and an obligation to introduce genuinely distinct practice.

$$\tau_R \gg \tau_P,\qquad P_k(t+\Delta)\in\{\text{renew},\text{mutate},\text{replace},\text{close}\}$$
A 15-year Port charter is a design conjecture, not a derived optimum.

Review should test institutional contribution, equity and distinct method—not cultural authenticity. “Remain foreign” cannot become a demand that people perform exotic difference for the host.

4 · formalising concordance

Joint action without a master metric.

Concordance is not a softer word for consensus. It is a decision architecture for cases where the participants may not share a complete ordering of outcomes.

4.1 Preference without compulsory commensuration

Let \(A\) be the set of possible joint actions and \(i\in\{1,\ldots,n\}\) the participating polities or affected publics. Each actor has a preference relation \(\succeq_i\) over at least some actions. The relations may be incomplete; not every loss can be translated into a common cardinal utility.

$$A_F=A_{\mathrm{physical}}\cap A_{\mathrm{rights}}\cap A_{\mathrm{budget}}\cap A_{\mathrm{deadline}}$$
Only physically possible, lawful, funded and timely actions enter the feasible set.

A decision procedure \(\Pi\) selects an executable action \(a^*\in A_F\). For every party \(i\), a dissent record \(d_i(a^*)\) preserves the reasons, losses, conditions or refusals that the operative decision does not resolve. Let \(\rho\) specify review, expiry and reopening rules.

$$\boxed{\mathcal{C}=\left(a^*,\Pi,D(a^*),\rho\right)},\qquad D(a^*)=(d_1,\ldots,d_n)$$
A concordance is an act, a legitimate procedure, preserved dissent and revisability.

4.2 Minimal conditions

ConditionRequirementWhat it prevents
Executability\(a^*\in A_F\) and can be implemented under the real deadline.Pluralism becoming endless discussion.
Procedural legitimacy\(\Pi\) is authorised, contestable and includes affected non-institutional publics.The strongest School defining the meeting.
Non-forced scalarisationNo common utility function is presumed where values are incommensurable.The Clear hiding political choices inside weights.

Consensus is a possible special case where relevant dissents disappear. It is neither required nor structurally preferred:

$$\text{Consensus}\subseteq\text{Concordance},\qquad \text{Concordance}\not\Rightarrow\text{agreement}$$

4.3 Optional decision aid: minimax regret

Where parties can responsibly express comparable, locally normalised losses, a minimax-regret calculation may help expose who is being asked to absorb the largest sacrifice:

$$a^{\dagger}=\arg\min_{a\in A_F}\max_i\left[\max_{b\in A_F}u_i(b)-u_i(a)\right]$$
This can inform \(\Pi\); it does not define Concordance and cannot represent every value.

The fictional Basin Seven Instrument is a stress test: it adopts a mixed solution under a deadline while annexing each unresolved refusal. The fiction demonstrates the form; it does not empirically validate the institution.

5 · formalising the interval

The protected outside is not the empty set.

Earlier shorthand wrote \(\varnothing\neq0\). The intuition was right; the mathematics was not yet precise.

Let \(L\) denote the relevant domain of childhood time, civic space and social practice. Let \(E\subset L\) be subject to compulsory educational programming or capture, and \(U\subset L\) be protected from such claims.

$$U\neq\varnothing,\qquad \mu(U)\ge u_{\min}>0,\qquad \operatorname{owner}_{\mathrm{edu}}(U)=\varnothing$$
The Interval has positive measure. What is absent is educational ownership—not care, law or relationship.

The formal notation matters. If (U) were literally empty, the Interval would be rhetoric. If (U) were wholly ungoverned, it could become a refuge for neglect or harm. Its special status is narrower: no School may compel it to justify itself as learning.

$$\forall s\in S:\quad \operatorname{capture}_s(U)=0,\qquad U\subseteq L_{\mathrm{safeguarded}}$$

Possible manifestations include unprogrammed portions of the day; civic spaces without compulsory educational analytics; family and community practices that may decline credentialling; land reserved without predetermined institutional use; and the right to participate in ordinary life without converting the experience into evidence.

The measurement paradox

A constitutional minimum normally requires measurement. But measuring the Minimum Unschoolable Life can make it a managed entitlement, then a performance indicator, then a ceiling. Evaluation must therefore prefer boundary audits—checking whether institutions crossed prohibited lines—over scoring the quality of the unprogrammed life itself.

Design proposition P2 · conjectural

A system needs an uncaptured outside in order to receive correction it did not generate.

This proposition connects Glissant’s opacity, Scott’s critique of legibility, Illich’s deschooling and the fictional knowledge of Ha Kim-lian and Roro. It remains a normative and empirical question: what kinds and quantities of outside-ness improve autonomy, critique and resilience, and under what safeguards?

6 · the clear as optimisation regime

It can prove that it is better.

The strongest antagonist to plural institutions is not authoritarian stupidity. It is a high-performing model with a comprehensible objective.

A unified planner might optimise a scalar objective over measurable outcomes (y_k(a)):

$$a_{\mathrm{Clear}}=\arg\max_{a\in A_F}J(a),\qquad J(a)=\sum_{k=1}^{K}\alpha_k y_k(a)-\lambda c(a)$$
Achievement, wellbeing, equity, efficiency and cost become one weighted score.

Nothing in this form is intrinsically wrong. Public decisions routinely require explicit priorities. The danger begins when (J) is mistaken for the whole public good, its weights become invisible, omitted values cannot file dissent, and short-run predictive accuracy becomes authority over long-run institutional extinction.

6.1 Institutional diversity as option coverage

Let \(\Omega\) be a set of plausible future disturbances and \(V_s(\omega)\) the response capacity retained by educational polity \(s\) under disturbance \(\omega\). One simple coverage measure is:

$$\operatorname{Cov}(S)=\sum_{\omega\in\Omega}p(\omega)\,\mathbf{1}\!\left[\exists s\in S:V_s(\omega)\ge\tau\right]$$
Does the portfolio retain at least one adequate response across plausible futures?

This formalises the option-value intuition, not a guarantee. Several systems may share the same blind spot. Variety can be cosmetic. A badly governed portfolio can preserve seven versions of the same model.

6.2 Diversity diagnostics

If (q_s) is the share of institutional capacity held by system (s), portfolio entropy and its effective number are:

$$H(S)=-\sum_s q_s\ln q_s,\qquad N_{\mathrm{eff}}=e^{H(S)}$$

Again, maximising entropy is not the objective. The relevant variety is functional: different error structures, evidence standards, pedagogic methods, time horizons and relationships to community. Two equally funded systems with identical models add little requisite variety.

Design proposition P3 · testable

The value of maintained variation increases with model uncertainty and irreversible institutional loss.

Where future environments are predictable and institutional forms easily recreated, central optimisation may dominate. Where uncertainty is high and lost lineages cannot be cheaply rebuilt, redundancy may have greater option value. The project must specify those conditions rather than declaring plurality universally superior.

7 · spatial architecture

Many centres, one Confluence, no educational reservation.

The physical system follows a developmental radius and a constitutional distinction between sediment, crossing, experiment and unassigned ground.

7.1 Educational radius by age

Let (r(a)) be the expected geographic radius of a learner’s ordinary educational activity at age (a). The conjecture is monotonic expansion, subject to strict travel-burden and accessibility constraints:

$$\frac{dr}{da}\ge0,\qquad \operatorname{travel}(a,q)\le B_a\quad\forall q$$
Older learners encounter more of the island; no group \(q\) should bear punitive travel to make the diagram work.

Neighbourhood Houses remain important, especially in primary years. Secondary learners move through Canton Colleges and Field Stations. Older learners acquire islandwide civic literacy. The model expands geography without abolishing local belonging.

7.3 The Confluence

A federal learning commons in the Bras Basah–Civic District is plausible because the area already contains deep layers of educational, civic and cultural history. It is not the eighth School. It holds shared archives, assembly, translation, teacher parliament, credential portability, residence and public debate.

At its centre lies (U_C), an open unprogrammed court:

$$U_C\subset U,\qquad \mu(U_C)>0,\qquad \forall s\in S:\operatorname{control}_s(U_C)=0$$
Seven entrances; no principal entrance; no permanent programme at the centre.

7.4 Future land as constitutional slack

For newly created territory, use a disjoint allocation:

$$L_{\mathrm{new}}=L_{\mathrm{needed}}\,\dot\cup\,L_{\mathrm{ecological}}\,\dot\cup\,L_{\mathrm{unassigned}},\qquad \mu(L_{\mathrm{unassigned}})>0$$
Unassigned does not mean abandoned; it means future generations retain programming authority.

The Greater Southern Waterfront can test mixing and shared infrastructure; Paya Lebar Air Base can test the \(5\times7\) crossing logic in a new town; Long Island can test revisable Port architectures inside a real water and climate system. These are design conjectures, not claims about adopted URA plans. Some offshore landscapes should remain outside institutional possession altogether.

8 · constitutional allocation

A thin centre still needs strong muscles.

“Thin” describes the range of doctrine it controls—not weakness in rights, finance, anti-segregation or emergency capacity.

The Commons must prevent the Seven from colonising the whole learning landscape. It therefore enforces not only a minimum educational entitlement but also a boundary beyond which educational institutions cannot compel legibility, optimisation or credentialling.

Provisional hypotheses

H1 · plurality

When educational polities retain genuine decision autonomy and cross every territorial canton, institutional variation increases without equivalent growth in postcode-based epistemic sorting.

H2 · resilience

Under high model uncertainty, a functionally diverse portfolio preserves more adequate response options across shocks than a single optimised system at comparable baseline resource levels.

H3 · concordance

Dissent-preserving instruments improve revision quality and institutional trust relative to decisions that record compromise as consensus, though they may reduce perceived decisiveness.

H4 · interval

Protected non-capture supports autonomy and external correction, but only when access to unprogrammed time and space is equitably distributed rather than privately purchased by the affluent.

10 · failure modes

The model’s enemies are not all called the Clear.

Most are ordinary consequences of institutional difference in a land-scarce, status-conscious city.

01

Plurality becomes class sorting

Families convert educational difference into a status market; prestige and housing follow.

Test: socioeconomic mutual information, travel burden, admissions advantage, housing price effects.

02

Roots become ethnic museums

Institutional ancestry hardens into racial expectation and authenticity policing.

Guardrail: universal enrolment, internal historical conflict, no ethnic ownership, language support across origins.

03

Ports become elite imports

Foreignness is sold as premium distinction rather than used as public experimentation.

Guardrail: common funding rules, public admissions, finite charters, explicit diffusion and failure review.

04

The Interval becomes affluent leisure

The privileged buy unstructured time while others receive surveillance and remedial optimisation.

Guardrail: equitable time and space floors; audit capture boundaries by class and disability.

05

Dissent annexes become theatre

Institutions file objections, gain moral cleanliness and continue unchanged.

Guardrail: annex-triggered review duties, public response, later outcome tracking.

06

The Confluence becomes a temple

A neutral commons acquires monumental prestige and quietly recentralises authority.

Guardrail: distributed federal functions, rotating assemblies, constitutional non-ownership of the centre.

07

Administrative navigation harms children

Adults enjoy the elegance of polycentricity; learners carry transfers, timetables and incompatible credentials.

Guardrail: one learner-facing interface, portability guarantees, developmental radius, navigation advocates.

08

Emergency recentralisation never ends

A crisis gives the Commons temporary power that becomes permanent.

Guardrail: automatic sunset, independent review, dissent preservation, restoration of devolved powers.

11 · research agenda

Do not begin by creating seven MOEs.

Begin by testing whether the architecture produces anything beyond more expensive administration and prettier institutions.

Historical and conceptual reconstruction

Map the actual institutional differences among Singapore’s former language streams without romanticising them. Trace governance, curriculum, social access, gender, funding, politics and reasons for convergence. Conduct close conceptual work on 和而不同, Concordance, opacity and the limit of schooling.

Formal and computational sandbox

Build a multi-layer network model of households, housing, transport, school choice, prestige, language and finance. Test when the \(5\times7\) structure cross-cuts identity and when it amplifies sorting. Model shocks under monocentric and polycentric governance with matched budgets.

Institutional protocol pilots

Pilot Concordance without creating new ministries: cross-institutional decisions that end in common operative clauses plus protected dissent annexes and scheduled reopening. Compare trust, implementation and learning against conventional consensus reporting.

Spatial prototype

Use one existing town centre to test shared civic-learning facilities after hours, distributed School presence and age-scaled mobility. Audit whether ordinary partners remain ordinary rather than becoming unpaid “learning resources.”

Interval experiment

Define a narrow, rights-compatible zone of non-capture—for example, limits on compulsory portfolio evidence, behavioural analytics or programming of specified public time. Evaluate boundary violations and autonomy qualitatively; do not optimise the Interval itself.

Constitutional scenario comparison

Only after evidence from the earlier phases: compare unitary, devolved, networked and fully polycentric models. Include administrative cost, equity, crisis response, institutional option value and the political legitimacy of maintained disagreement.

Most useful near-term artefact

A simulation and governance protocol—not a masterplan. The project’s first empirical contribution could be a transparent model showing the conditions under which cross-cutting educational autonomy reduces or worsens sorting, paired with a real-world test of dissent-preserving joint decisions.

Conclusion · provisional theorem of the project

Plurality needs architecture, cost and a limit.

Difference without architecture becomes branding. Architecture without equity becomes enclosure. Equity without maintained variation can become a perfectly fair route into one model. Variation without common action becomes paralysis. Common action without preserved dissent becomes false consensus. And a society where every experience is absorbed as education loses the outside from which education can be corrected.

$$\boxed{\mathfrak{L}=\left(T\times(R\cup P)\right)\oplus F\oplus U}$$
A living educational commonwealth: crossings of place and School, a federal Commons, and a protected Interval.

The formula is not a solution. It is a compact research programme. Its worth will depend on whether it can survive history, simulation, cost, children, politics, ordinary life—and the people who refuse to fit inside it.
