The Seven Operations & The Grand Measure

Beneath conventional subjects lie deeper intellectual operations: limit, axiom, form, substrate, law, expression and intervention. The Great Schools absorb them into different theories of what knowing is — and once every few years, they meet in a competition where only the parameters are shared.

Below the disciplines

The familiar school subject is only one historical container for a deeper operation.

Physical education, mathematics, biology, chemistry, physics, the arts and design can be understood as recurrent ways of interrogating reality. This makes them portable across the Seven Schools.

Discipline ≠ natural category
Discipline ≈ institutional container for an epistemic operation
Seven verbs of knowing

endure · relate · live · transform · constrain · express · intervene

The Schools can therefore share access to the same reality while disagreeing radically about how it should be partitioned.

01

TKI — Operations as Frames

What had to be framed this way for this knowledge to become possible?

PE becomes the body as frame and limit. Mathematics becomes declared assumptions. Biology asks where organism ends and environment begins. Physics studies models that claim independence from perspective. Art is explicit framing. Design becomes deliberate reframing.

body as frameaxiomatic assumptionsboundary drawing model dependencereframing

A TKI mathematics class changes axioms to see which conclusions survive. A biology lesson redraws the same system from organism, ecosystem and molecular perspectives. PE might remove sight, speech or individual mobility to expose how “ability” depends on the frame.

02

Amanah — Operations as Warranted Capability

Can you demonstrate why you should be trusted to do this?

PE asks when competence has genuinely been earned. Mathematics models explicit justification. Biology and chemistry introduce obligations toward living systems and dangerous transformations. Physics becomes trustworthy measurement. Art becomes transmission with lineage. Design becomes authorised intervention.

Gymnasium, laboratory, studio and clinic all operate through apprenticeship, witness and competency. At graduation one is entrusted with a capacity.

witnesscompetencyprovenancecustody
03

Sri Temenggong — Operations Inside Continuity

What relationships allow this knowledge to remain alive?

The seven operations remain present, but never become isolated shelves. Mathematics is encountered in recurrence; chemistry in soil, fermentation and dye; physics in tide, heat and wind; biology in ecology; art in memory; design in careful modification of continuity.

Their equivalent of “Chemistry I” might simply be The Estuary: salinity, hydrodynamics, mangrove biology, fishing, boat-building, statistics, songs, law, memory and intervention held together as one living field.

04

Lam Keng — Operations as Operations

What transformation is occurring?

PE becomes optimisation of bodily capacity. Mathematics is the pure grammar of transformation. Biology becomes morphogenesis and replication. Chemistry is paradigmatic transformation. Physics supplies invariants. Art is composition. Design is orchestration.

repeattransformstabilise amplifycompresscompose

The same operation might be studied through equations in the morning, metallurgy at noon, dance in the afternoon and architecture in the evening. Disciplinary identity matters less than transferable operational grammar.

05

Nine Rains — Operations as Flow

What enters, what leaves, what accumulates and what returns?

PE becomes oxygen, energy, attention and coordinated effort. Mathematics becomes rates and gradients. Biology becomes metabolism. Chemistry becomes reaction pathways. Physics becomes transfer. Art becomes circulation of affect and meaning. Design becomes intervention into flows.

Traffic congestion, insulin regulation, capital concentration, flooding, heat transfer and public attention are different phenomena that share a grammar of bottleneck, storage, feedback, threshold and return.

06

Kora — Operations as Claims

What kind of account is this, and what can it legitimately claim?

PE asks what a body can testify to. Mathematics becomes explicit claim structure. Biology exposes competing taxonomies. Chemistry foregrounds reproducibility. Physics foregrounds instrumentation. Art proves that representation is never identical to experience. Design negotiates incompatible definitions of the problem.

Kora does not collapse all knowledge into mere opinion. It trains students to distinguish radically different kinds of account while never confusing the account with reality itself.

07

Sund — Operations as Collective Capability

What must a society actually be able to do?

PE asks what bodies and groups can practically accomplish. Mathematics enables allocation and coordination. Biology sustains health and food. Chemistry enables sanitation and materials. Physics supports infrastructure. Art produces shared meaning. Design integrates all of them into usable conditions.

To understand water means being able to measure, move, clean, allocate, conserve, repair, govern and communicate it. Knowledge that cannot enter collective capability remains incomplete.

The inter-school mega-games

The Grand Measure

The Schools cannot agree on subjects. They cannot agree on epistemology. They cannot even fully agree on what excellence means. So the Games are built around something narrower: shared parameters.

No School defines the method.
The organisers define only the boundary conditions, permissible resources, measurement and end-state.
The neutral territory

Method is free. Parameters are strict. Outcomes are measured. Interpretation comes afterward.

conditions + constraints + measurement → comparable result
Bodily event

The Common Pulse

Seven participants must move themselves and a fixed mass through a complex course. Each begins with different sensory, informational or physical constraints.

Measured:
  • completion time
  • errors and penalties
  • energy expenditure
  • injury / safety
  • load integrity

Nobody possesses enough information or capacity to complete the course alone.

Axiomatic event

The Closed World

Teams receive a completely novel formal system: a small set of axioms, symbols and legal transformations invented for the Games.

Task:
  • derive what follows
  • identify what is undecidable
  • detect contradiction if present
  • construct the most complex valid structure possible

A universe whose laws were invented that morning: what can be true here?

Physical event

The Constraint Engine

Every team receives identical material mass, volume, tools, energy allowance and time.

Task: move a fixed mass vertically to a precise target location.
score ≈ useful work / (energy × material mass) × precision

Physics, geometry, fabrication, coordination and strategic interpretation collapse into one measurable challenge.

Material event

Matter

Teams receive the same safe starting materials and target properties, and design their own object.

Example requirement:
  • support a defined load
  • remain below a thermal threshold
  • survive a set number of cycles
  • return cleanly to constituent streams afterward

The rules committee becomes almost as famous as the competitors.

Living systems event

Persistence

Each team receives an identical closed ecological system with fixed water, light, volume, organisms, energy and time.

Objective: maintain viable biological function while producing a required output without destabilising the system.
score ≈ output + stability + diversity − external input
Representational event

Representation

Competitors experience something unavailable to the audience, then construct an artefact or performance that transmits something of that experience.

Measured across blinded viewers:
  • semantic transmission
  • affective correspondence
  • memory persistence
  • interpretive variance

The scoring controversy is itself part of the event: is low variance clarity, or merely conformity?

The supreme event

Intervention

A temporary settlement is built for the Games. Each team receives the same land area, population profile, resource envelope, climate data, budget and construction period. Then the organisers reveal a live systems challenge.

water failureheat shockmass relocation energy constraintmobility disruption

Teams must intervene. The settlement then operates. No jury decides whether the answer is “beautiful.” Consequences are measured.

Why the Games matter

The Schools cannot hide inside their own definitions of excellence.

TKI cannot merely argue that the framing is wrong. Amanah cannot stop at proper authority. Sri Temenggong cannot preserve continuity indefinitely. Lam Keng cannot transform forever. Nine Rains cannot only map the system. Kora cannot remain with unresolved accounts. Sund cannot rely only on robustness.

The clock starts. The boundary conditions are declared. Something has to happen.

And afterward, naturally, the Seven Schools spend the next four years arguing about what the result actually proved.
