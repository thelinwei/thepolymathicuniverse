01 · The settled name

Lan Fang is the current school name.

Use Lan Fang consistently from this decision onward. The working full form is Lan Fang College.

This supersedes Lam Keng, Jit Sin, Lan Fong and Lam Fong as the school’s current editorial label. Those forms remain part of the development record; retaining any of them as an in-world historical name requires a separate authorial decision.

Chinese name: the traditional and simplified forms above. A possible design convention is to use traditional characters on ceremonial inscriptions and simplified characters in contemporary publications.

02 · The shared naming principle

One public name. Several layers of belonging.

Lin Wei has approved secondary internal names in the institutions’ own languages and symbols. These names can carry distinct histories, relationships and meanings; they need not be literal translations of the public name.

A school may have a public identity, a name its own community uses, and names preserved in its inscriptions, ceremonies and inherited records.

The naming structure is agreed. The individual secondary names below are suggestions and retained working forms, each awaiting its own decision.

04 · Scripts, accents and cultural specificity

Let each mark carry meaning.

Language before decoration

Use accents, umlauts and other marks when they carry pronunciation, language or inherited spelling. Their presence should tell us something about the name.

Specific lineages

Develop further native names for Nine Rain, Kora and Sund from their particular linguistic lineages. Each institution deserves a precise cultural and linguistic history.

The institutions can be multilingual. A public name, an internal name and a ceremonial inscription may belong to different languages, reflecting the people who founded, sustained and changed the school.

05 · A later chapter

Invented languages and symbols can come later.

Lin Wei has explicitly left room for new symbols and languages in the world’s future development.

Existing languages and scripts give this chapter its foundation. Invented languages and symbols belong to a later chapter.
