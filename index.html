On the numbering

Each volume keeps the number it was given when it was made, so the numbers run in the order of the work, 00 to 131. Volumes made by different hands carried their own atlas numbers, which stay on each title page. Atlas IX·a, The Gloss (Claude, 17 September), is still to be recovered from its original conversation.

On the books and labels

The four books sort the volumes by what they are: the designs, the world, the magic, and the research to follow up. Under each book a small italic label says what a volume covers. The newest or most settled volumes lead each label as cards, and the earlier ones sit in the list beneath.

On the names

Volumes before 73 keep the names they were made with: Toa Keng Institution, Al-Merikani College, Sri Temenggong Institution (Balai Pusaka within its own walls), Lan Fang College 兰芳书院, the House of Nine Rain, the Sané-Kora Institute and the Sund Commons. From volume 73 each house carries a second, chosen name, and from there names, identities and outcomes keep moving, each volume holding the names of its own moment.
