On the numbering

Volumes made by different hands carried their own atlas numbers, so the archive holds two Atlas VIIIs, two IXs and two magical series. The two-digit numbers here are the reading order; the original numbers stay on each title page.

On the names

Every volume uses the settled register: Toa Keng Institution, Al-Merikani College, Sri Temenggong Institution (Balai Pusaka within its own walls), Lan Fang College 兰芳书院, the House of Nine Rain, the Sané-Kora Institute and the Sund Commons. Lan Fang takes its name from the Lanfang kongsi of west Borneo, 1777–1884; volumes 67 and 68 record the decision.

Still to recover

Atlas IX·a, The Gloss (Claude, 17 September), lives in its original conversation, ready to be rebuilt with the settled names.
